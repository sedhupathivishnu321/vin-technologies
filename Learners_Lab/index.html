What you can do

Electronics

Drag parts onto a breadboard, wire them with editable jumpers and run. LEDs glow with the simulated current, buzzers and speakers play through your PC, motors spin.

Arduino

Write C/C++ sketches (a practical subset) and run them against the circuit: digital and analog I/O, PWM, tone(), Serial output.

Live sliders

Every adjustable value has a slider. Turn on Live and LEDs, meters, the oscilloscope and graphs follow as you drag.

Oscilloscope

Volts and time per division, edge trigger, cursors, automatic measurements, XY, FFT and math channels. The waveform viewer adds statistics, overlay and CSV export.

Lab experiments

Ready-made, fully wired experiments with objective, theory, steps and automatic checks against the textbook result.

Networks

Hosts, hubs, switches, access points, routers and firewalls. Simulate ARP, ping, traceroute, UDP, TCP, DNS and DHCP, then replay every packet and decode it.

Trainer Kits

8085 and 8051 kits with assembler, step debugger, LCD, keypad, motors, DAC/ADC and 74 checked experiments.

Logic from components

NOT, NAND, NOR, AND, OR and XOR built from diodes, transistors and MOSFETs (RTL, DTL, TTL, CMOS), plus adders and latches.

Specifications

  • Workspaces: Electronics (breadboard, Arduino sketches, 49 parts, bench instruments, oscilloscope), Network (hosts, switches, routers, firewalls, packet replay), Circuits Pro (197 parts, 79 lab experiments) and Trainer Kits (8085 and 8051, 74 experiments).
  • Lab experiments: about 90 wired experiments with objective, theory, steps and automatic checks: Basic Electrical, Electronic Devices, Analog, Digital, Microcontroller and Computer Networks.
  • Engine: circuit solver in native C++ (Windows build); Arduino C/C++ subset interpreter; discrete-event network simulator.
  • Files: projects save as .ecelab.json and .ecelab.net.json; results export to CSV and images.
  • Privacy: works fully offline, no sign-in, no tracking.
