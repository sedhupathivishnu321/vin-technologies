LEARNERS LAB App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026. Applies to the LEARNERS LAB desktop app for Windows (version 1.1.1 and later), published by VIN Technologies. The website is covered by the separate website Privacy Policy.

In short

The app works completely offline. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics or crash reporting, and no advertising. VIN Technologies does not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data from the app.

What stays on your computer

These files are created and kept only on your own device:

  • Your projects: circuits and networks you save are written to the location you choose; the default folder is Documents\LEARNERS LAB\Projects.
  • Settings: theme, autosave interval, recent files and the dates used to decide when the support note is shown, in %USERPROFILE%\.ecelab\settings.json.
  • Recovery copy: an autosaved copy of unsaved work, offered back to you after an unexpected close and deleted when you exit normally.
  • Circuits Pro and Trainer Kits data: these workspaces run inside the app from local files. Their programs, circuits and preferences are kept in the app's own local storage folder under %APPDATA%. No cookies are kept between sessions.
  • Exports: CSV files, images and downloads are saved only where you tell the app to save them.

You can delete all of this at any time by deleting those folders or uninstalling the app.

Network use

The app does not send your projects, files or usage information anywhere. The built-in workspaces are served from your own computer on a loopback address (127.0.0.1) that other devices cannot reach. The simulated networks are models inside the app; they do not send real traffic on your network.

The only time the app contacts anything outside your computer is when you click an external link, such as Buy me a coffee in the Support window. That opens your default web browser, and the site you visit then applies its own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or accounts. It plays sound through your speakers for simulated buzzers and speakers, and reads and writes the files you open and save.

Support option

The Support window shows a UPI QR code and a link to Buy me a coffee. Donations are optional and are handled entirely by your UPI app or by Buy me a coffee. VIN Technologies does not see your payment details through the app.

Children

The app is an educational tool for students. It collects no information from anyone, including children.
