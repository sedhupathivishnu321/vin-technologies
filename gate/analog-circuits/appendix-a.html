A.1 Logarithms

\(\log_{10}(ab)=\log a+\log b\); \(\log(a/b)=\log a-\log b\); \(\log a^n=n\log a\); \(\ln x=2.303\log_{10}x\). Useful values: \(\log2=0.301\), \(\log3=0.477\), \(\log5=0.699\), \(\log7=0.845\), \(\ln2=0.693\), \(\ln10=2.303\).

A.2 Decibels

\(\mathrm{dB}=20\log_{10}(V_2/V_1)=10\log_{10}(P_2/P_1)\).   \(-3\) dB \(\leftrightarrow0.707\); \(+6\) dB \(\leftrightarrow2\); \(+20\) dB \(\leftrightarrow10\); \(+40\) dB \(\leftrightarrow100\). dBm \(=10\log(P/1\,\mathrm{mW})\).

A.3 Complex numbers

\(z=a+jb=re^{j\theta}\), \(r=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\), \(\theta=\tan^{-1}(b/a)\). \(|z_1z_2|=|z_1||z_2|\), \(\angle(z_1z_2)=\theta_1+\theta_2\). \(j^2=-1\), \(1/j=-j\), \(e^{j\theta}=\cos\theta+j\sin\theta\).

A.4 Phasors

\(v(t)=V_m\cos(\omega t+\phi)\leftrightarrow\mathbf V=V_me^{j\phi}\). Impedances: \(R\); \(j\omega L\); \(1/(j\omega C)\). RMS: \(V_{rms}=V_m/\sqrt2\).

A.5 Differential equations

First-order RC: \(\tau\dot y+y=x\); step response \(y=x_f(1-e^{-t/\tau})\); general: \(y(t)=y(\infty)+[y(0)-y(\infty)]e^{-t/\tau}\); \(10\)–\(90\%\) rise time \(=2.2\tau\); \(t_{50\%}=0.69\tau\). Second-order: \(\ddot y+2\zeta\omega_0\dot y+\omega_0^2y=\omega_0^2x\).

A.6 Laplace transforms

\(f(t)\)\(F(s)\)
\(\delta(t)\)1
\(u(t)\)\(1/s\)
\(t\,u(t)\)\(1/s^2\)
\(e^{-at}u(t)\)\(1/(s+a)\)
\(\sin\omega t\)\(\omega/(s^2+\omega^2)\)
\(\cos\omega t\)\(s/(s^2+\omega^2)\)
\(\dot f\)\(sF-f(0)\)
\(\int f\)\(F/s\)

A.7 Fourier concepts

Periodic signal \(=\) sum of harmonics. Full-wave rectified sine: \(|\sin\omega t|=\dfrac{2}{\pi}-\dfrac4\pi\sum\dfrac{\cos2k\omega t}{4k^2-1}\); square wave (odd harmonics, amplitudes \(\propto1/n\)). Average \(=a_0\); RMS \(=\sqrt{a_0^2+\sum V_n^2/2}\). Bandwidth of a pulse \(\approx1/\tau_{pulse}\).

A.8 Transfer functions

\(H(s)=N(s)/D(s)\); frequency response \(H(j\omega)\); poles are roots of \(D\), zeros are roots of \(N\); \(H(0)\) is the DC gain, \(H(\infty)\) the high-frequency gain; stable iff all poles have \(\mathrm{Re}<0\).
