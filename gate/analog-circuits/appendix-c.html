C.1 One-page diode revision

Diode in 60 seconds

\(I=I_s(e^{V/nV_T}-1)\); \(V_T=26\) mV; \(\times10\) current \(\to+60\) mV; \(r_d=26/I(\mathrm{mA})\) \(\Omega\); \(-2\) mV/\({}^\circ\)C; \(C_j\propto(V_R)^{-1/2}\); \(C_d\propto I\).
Method: assume ON/OFF \(\to\) solve \(\to\) check \(I>0\) / \(V<0.7\).
Clipper level \(=V_R\pm0.7\); clamper \(V_C=V_m\pm V_R-0.7\); HWR \(V_m/\pi\), FWR \(2V_m/\pi\); ripple \(1.21/0.482\); PIV \(V_m\) (bridge), \(2V_m\) (CT); filter \(V_r=I/(f_rC)\).

C.2 One-page BJT revision

BJT in 60 seconds

Active: BE fwd, BC rev. \(I_C=\beta I_B\), \(I_E=(\beta+1)I_B\), \(V_{BE}=0.7\), \(V_{CE(sat)}=0.2\).
Bias: divider stiff \(\Rightarrow I_E=(V_B-0.7)/R_E\). \(S=\dfrac{(\beta+1)(1+R_B/R_E)}{\beta+1+R_B/R_E}\).
\(g_m=I_C/V_T\); \(r_\pi=\beta/g_m\); \(r_e=1/g_m\); \(r_o=V_A/I_C\).
CE: \(-g_mR_C'\); CB: \(+g_mR_C'\), \(R_{in}=r_e\); CC: \(\approx1\), \(R_{out}=(r_\pi+R_s)/(\beta+1)\). Unbypassed \(R_E\): \(-R_C'/(r_e+R_E)\).
\(f_H\): \(C_\pi+C_\mu(1+g_mR_L')\); \(f_T=g_m/2\pi(C_\pi+C_\mu)\).

C.3 One-page MOSFET revision

MOSFET in 60 seconds

\(V_{ov}=V_{GS}-V_{th}\); sat if \(V_{DS}\ge V_{ov}\): \(I_D=\tfrac12k_nV_{ov}^2(1+\lambda V_{DS})\); triode: \(k_n[V_{ov}V_{DS}-V_{DS}^2/2]\).
\(g_m=\sqrt{2k_nI_D}=2I_D/V_{ov}\); \(r_o=1/\lambda I_D\); \(g_{mb}=\chi g_m\).
CS \(-g_mR_D'\); CG \(+g_mR_D'\), \(R_{in}=1/g_m\); CD \(g_mR_L'/(1+g_mR_L')\), \(R_{out}=1/g_m\). Degeneration \(-g_mR_D'/(1+g_mR_S)\).
Body effect raises \(V_{th}\) when \(V_{SB}>0\). Bias quadratic: discard root with \(V_{GS}<V_{th}\).

C.4 One-page current-mirror revision

Mirrors in 60 seconds

BJT \(I_O=I_{REF}/(1+2/\beta)\); MOS \(I_O=I_{REF}(W/L)_2/(W/L)_1\); \(R_O=r_o\).
Widlar \(I_OR_E=V_T\ln(I_{REF}/I_O)\); Wilson error \(\approx2/\beta^2\), \(R_O\approx\beta r_o/2\); cascode \(R_O\approx g_mr_o^2\), compliance \(V_{th}+2V_{ov}\).
Active load gain \(=g_m(r_{on}\|r_{op})\).

C.5 One-page differential-amplifier revision

Diff-amp in 60 seconds

\(v_{id}=v_1-v_2\); \(v_{ic}=(v_1+v_2)/2\). Half circuits: DM (virtual ground) and CM (\(2R_{EE}\)).
\(A_d=g_mR_C\) (diff-out); single-ended \(=g_mR_C/2\); \(R_{id}=2r_\pi\). \(A_{cm}=-R_C/2R_{EE}\); CMRR \(=g_mR_{EE}\).
MOS: all current steers at \(\sqrt2V_{ov}\); mirror load \(A_d=g_m(r_o\|r_o)\). \(V_{OS}\approx\Delta V_{th}\); ICMR set by tail compliance and load.

C.6 One-page op-amp revision

Op-amp in 60 seconds

Rules: \(v_+=v_-\), \(i_\pm=0\) (negative feedback only).
Inv \(-R_f/R_1\) (\(R_{in}=R_1\)); non-inv \(1+R_f/R_1\); buffer 1; diff \((R_2/R_1)(v_2-v_1)\); summer \(-R_f\sum v_k/R_k\); IA \((1+2R/R_G)\).
Integrator \(-\int v_i/RC\); diff \(-RC\,\dot v_i\); \(f_{FP}=SR/2\pi V_p\); BW \(=\) GBW\(/\)noise gain; \(V_{o,OS}=(1+R_f/R_1)V_{OS}\).
Comparator: \(\pm V_{sat}\); Schmitt inv \(\pm\beta V_{sat}\), non-inv \(\pm(R_1/R_2)V_{sat}\).

C.7 One-page filter revision

Filters in 60 seconds

\(f_c=1/2\pi RC\); Butterworth \(|H|^2=1/[1+(f/f_c)^{2n}]\), \(-20n\) dB/dec; \(Q=0.707\) for \(n=2\).
BPF \(f_0=\sqrt{f_Lf_H}\), \(Q=f_0/B\); notch \(B=f_0/Q\); all-pass \(\phi=-2\tan^{-1}\omega RC\).
SK (equal): \(Q=1/(3-K)\); SK unity-gain LP: \(Q=\sqrt{R_1R_2C_1C_2}/[C_2(R_1+R_2)]\).
MFB: \(Q=\pi f_0R_3C\), \(A_0=-R_3/2R_1\). Chebyshev: ripple, steeper, poles on an ellipse.

C.8 One-page oscillator revision

Oscillators in 60 seconds

Barkhausen: \(|A\beta|=1\), \(\angle=0\); start-up \(>1\).
RC phase-shift \(f=1/(2\pi RC\sqrt6)\), \(A\ge29\); Wien \(f=1/2\pi RC\), \(A\ge3\).
Colpitts \(f=1/2\pi\sqrt{LC_1C_2/(C_1+C_2)}\); Hartley \(1/2\pi\sqrt{(L_1+L_2+2M)C}\); crystal \(f_s<f_p\), \(Q\sim10^5\).
Triangle generator \(f=R_2/(4R_1RC)\).

C.9 One-page stability revision

Stability in 60 seconds

\(A_f=A/(1+A\beta)\); PM \(=180+\angle L(\omega_{gc})\); GM \(=-20\log|L(\omega_{pc})|\); PM \(\ge45^\circ\), GM \(\ge10\) dB.
\(\zeta\approx\) PM\(/100\); overshoot: \(30^\circ\to42\%\), \(45^\circ\to23\%\), \(60^\circ\to9\%\).
Follower PM \(=90-\tan^{-1}(\text{GBW}/f_2)\). Rate of closure \(\le20\) dB/dec. Miller \(C_c\): \(p_1\downarrow\), \(p_2\uparrow\), \(\omega_t=g_{m1}/C_c\); RHP zero \(g_{m2}/C_c\).
