D.1 Must-know concepts

Diode ON/OFF analysis; rectifier parameters; BJT/MOS region tests and DC bias; small-signal parameters and the six single-stage amplifiers; current mirrors and diff-pair half circuits; op-amp golden rules and standard circuits; comparators and Schmitt thresholds; filter order and \(Q\); oscillator frequencies and gain conditions; feedback topologies; Bode-plot construction, PM and GM.

D.2 Must-remember equations

\(V_T=26\) mV; \(g_m=I_C/V_T\) (BJT) and \(2I_D/V_{ov}\) (MOS); \(r_\pi=\beta/g_m\); \(r_o=V_A/I_C\); \(A_v=-g_mR_L'\); \(A_f=A/(1+A\beta)\); \(f_c=1/2\pi RC\); \(C_M=C(1+|A|)\); \(f_T=g_m/2\pi(C_\pi+C_\mu)\); \(V_{dc}=V_m/\pi\) or \(2V_m/\pi\); \(V_{UT}=\beta V_{sat}\); PM \(=180^\circ+\angle L\).

D.3 Frequently tested concepts

Two-diode circuits; biased clippers; BJT with divider bias; CE with/without \(R_E\) bypass; source-follower gain; mirror ratio; CMRR; op-amp with saturation; Schmitt hysteresis width; Sallen–Key \(Q\); Wien/Colpitts frequency; feedback topology type; PM of a follower.

D.4 Common calculation errors

Using \(I_C\) for \(I_E\) in the base loop; forgetting \((\beta+1)R_E\); using \(V_{BE}=0.7\) for a PNP with wrong sign; quoting single-ended gain as differential (factor 2); dropping \(R_L\) in \(R_C\|R_L\); mixing rad/s and Hz; omitting \(\times20\) or \(\times10\) in dB; wrong quadratic root in MOS bias; using \(V_{sat}=\) supply when a value is given.

D.5 Common conceptual traps

Virtual short in a comparator; \(r_d\) depends on \(I_D\) not \(V_D\); clamper does not clip; PIV with a filter capacitor is \(2V_m\); Miller effect only for inverting stages; body effect only when \(V_{SB}\neq0\); feedback does not improve input-referred SNR; a Schmitt trigger does not filter noise; integrators need a DC path.

D.6 24-hour revision sheet

Hour 0–4: formula book (Ch. 30) and one-page sheets (App. C).
Hour 4–10: solve Chapter 31 (topic-wise) without notes; mark mistakes.
Hour 10–14: Level 2 and Level 3 tests (Ch. 33).
Hour 14–18: redo mistakes; re-derive: Miller, Widlar, Sallen–Key \(Q\), \(S\) factor.
Hour 18–22: traps (App. D.5), MSQ drill, sleep.
Hour 22–24: light formula recall only.

D.8 Final Analog Circuits formula sheet

Print or copy Chapter 30 and the one-page sheets of Appendix C; carry them in your final revision. Pay special attention to derived quantities that GATE likes to disguise: \(g_m\) from \(I_C\) or \((k_n,I_D)\), effective load \(R_C\|R_L\|r_o\), reflected resistance \((\beta+1)R_E\), noise gain \(1+R_f/R_1\), closed-loop bandwidth GBW\(/(1+A\beta)\)-type relations, and PM from pole positions.
