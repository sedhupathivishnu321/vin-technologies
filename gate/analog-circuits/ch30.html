30.1 Diode formulas

Diode

\(I_D=I_s(e^{V_D/nV_T}-1)\), \(V_T=kT/q=26\) mV    \(\Delta V=60\) mV per decade of \(I_D\)
\(r_d=nV_T/I_D\)    \(C_j=\dfrac{C_{j0}}{(1+V_R/V_{bi})^m}\)    \(C_d=\tau_TI_D/(nV_T)\)
\(V_{bi}=V_T\ln\dfrac{N_AN_D}{n_i^2}\)    \(W\propto\sqrt{V_{bi}+V_R}\)    \(dV_D/dT=-2\) mV/\({}^\circ\)C
Models: ideal (\(0\) V), CV (\(0.7\) V), PWL (\(V_{on}+r_dI\)). Clippers: shunt clip level \(=V_R\pm V_{on}\).
Clamper: \(V_C=V_m\pm V_R-V_{on}\); tilt \(\approx1/(2fR_LC)\) (square wave).

30.3 BJT formulas

BJT

\(I_E=I_C+I_B\); \(I_C=\beta I_B\); \(\alpha=\beta/(\beta+1)\); \(I_C=I_Se^{V_{BE}/V_T}(1+V_{CE}/V_A)\)
Biasing: fixed \(I_B=\dfrac{V_{CC}-V_{BE}}{R_B}\); C2B \(I_B=\dfrac{V_{CC}-V_{BE}}{R_B+(\beta+1)R_C}\); emitter \(I_B=\dfrac{V_{CC}-V_{BE}}{R_B+(\beta+1)R_E}\); divider \(I_B=\dfrac{V_{TH}-V_{BE}}{R_{TH}+(\beta+1)R_E}\)
Stability: fixed \(S=\beta+1\); \(S=\dfrac{(\beta+1)(1+R_B/R_E)}{\beta+1+R_B/R_E}\)
Small signal: \(g_m=I_C/V_T\); \(r_\pi=\beta/g_m\); \(r_e=\alpha/g_m\); \(r_o=V_A/I_C\); \(r_\pi=(\beta+1)r_e\); \(g_mr_o=V_A/V_T\)

30.4 MOSFET formulas

NMOS

\(V_{ov}=V_{GS}-V_{th}\); Triode (\(V_{DS}<V_{ov}\)): \(I_D=k_n[V_{ov}V_{DS}-V_{DS}^2/2]\); Saturation: \(I_D=\tfrac12k_nV_{ov}^2(1+\lambda V_{DS})\), \(k_n=\mu_nC_{ox}W/L\)
\(g_m=k_nV_{ov}=\sqrt{2k_nI_D}=2I_D/V_{ov}\); \(r_o=1/\lambda I_D\); \(g_{mb}=\chi g_m\), \(\chi=\dfrac{\gamma}{2\sqrt{2\phi_F+V_{SB}}}\)
\(V_{th}=V_{th0}+\gamma(\sqrt{2\phi_F+V_{SB}}-\sqrt{2\phi_F})\); \(f_T=\dfrac{g_m}{2\pi(C_{gs}+C_{gd})}\)
Depletion: \(I_D=I_{DSS}(1-V_{GS}/V_P)^2\)

30.6 Current-mirror formulas

Key Formulae

BJT: \(I_O=\dfrac{I_{REF}}{1+2/\beta}\); MOS: \(I_O=I_{REF}\dfrac{(W/L)_2}{(W/L)_1}\); \(R_O=r_o\)
Widlar: \(I_OR_E=V_T\ln(I_{REF}/I_O)\); Wilson: \(I_O\approx I_{REF}(1-2/\beta^2)\), \(R_O\approx\beta r_o/2\); Cascode MOS: \(R_O\approx g_mr_o^2\)
Active load: \(A_v=-g_m(r_{on}\|r_{op})\)

30.7 Differential-amplifier formulas

Key Formulae

\(v_{id}=v_1-v_2\), \(v_{ic}=(v_1+v_2)/2\); BJT pair: \(A_d=g_mR_C\) (diff. out), \(g_mR_C/2\) (single-ended), \(R_{id}=2r_\pi\)
\(A_{cm}\approx-R_C/2R_{EE}\); CMRR \(\approx g_mR_{EE}\); mirror load: \(A_d=g_m(r_{o2}\|r_{o4})\)
MOS: \(|v_{id}|_{max}=\sqrt2V_{ov}\); \(V_{OS}\approx\Delta V_{th}\); BJT \(V_{OS}\approx V_T\Delta R_C/R_C\)

30.9 Filter formulas

Key Formulae

1st order: \(f_c=1/2\pi RC\); Butterworth: \(|H|^2=1/[1+(f/f_c)^{2n}]\), \(-20n\) dB/dec
BPF: \(f_0=\sqrt{f_Lf_H}\), \(Q=f_0/B\); notch: \(B=f_0/Q\); all-pass: \(\phi=-2\tan^{-1}\omega RC\)
Sallen–Key LP (unity gain): \(\omega_0=1/\sqrt{R_1R_2C_1C_2}\), \(Q=\sqrt{R_1R_2C_1C_2}/[C_2(R_1+R_2)]\); with gain \(K\): \(Q=1/(3-K)\)
MFB BPF: \(f_0=\dfrac{1}{2\pi C}\sqrt{\dfrac{R_1+R_2}{R_1R_2R_3}}\), \(Q=\pi f_0R_3C\), \(A_0=-R_3/2R_1\)
Order: \(n\ge\dfrac{\log[(10^{A_s/10}-1)/(10^{A_p/10}-1)]}{2\log(f_s/f_p)}\)

30.10 Oscillator formulas

Key Formulae

Barkhausen: \(|A\beta|=1\), \(\angle A\beta=0\).
RC phase-shift: \(f_0=\dfrac{1}{2\pi RC\sqrt6}\), \(|A|\ge29\)    Wien: \(f_0=\dfrac{1}{2\pi RC}\), \(A\ge3\)
Colpitts: \(f_0=\dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC_1C_2/(C_1+C_2)}}\), \(A\ge C_2/C_1\)    Hartley: \(f_0=\dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{(L_1+L_2+2M)C}}\), \(A\ge L_1/L_2\)
Crystal: \(f_s=\dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{L_sC_s}}\), \(f_p=f_s\sqrt{1+C_s/C_0}\)
Relaxation (triangle): \(f=\dfrac{R_2}{4R_1RC}\)

30.11 Feedback formulas

Key Formulae

\(A_f=\dfrac{A}{1+A\beta}\); \(\dfrac{dA_f}{A_f}=\dfrac{1}{1+A\beta}\dfrac{dA}{A}\); \(f_{Hf}=f_H(1+A\beta)\); \(f_{Lf}=f_L/(1+A\beta)\); \(D_f=D/(1+A\beta)\)
Series mixing: \(R_{if}=R_i(1+A\beta)\); shunt: \(R_i/(1+A\beta)\). Voltage sampling: \(R_{of}=R_o/(1+A\beta)\); current: \(R_o(1+A\beta)\)

30.12 Frequency-response formulas

Key Formulae

\(f_{Lk}=\dfrac1{2\pi R_kC_k}\); overall \(f_L\approx\sqrt{\sum f_{Lk}^2}\); \(f_H\approx\dfrac{1}{2\pi\sum\tau_k}\); \(n\) identical stages: \(f_H\sqrt{2^{1/n}-1}\)
Miller: \(C_{in}=C_F(1+|A|)\); \(f_T=\dfrac{g_m}{2\pi(C_\pi+C_\mu)}\); \(f_\beta=f_T/\beta_0\); GBW\(=|A|f_H\)
2nd order: \(Q=1/2\zeta\); \(M_p=1/(2\zeta\sqrt{1-\zeta^2})\); pole: \(-45^\circ\) at \(\omega_p\), \(-5.7^\circ\) at \(0.1\omega_p\)

30.13 Stability formulas

Key Formulae

\(1+L(s)=0\); \(\mathrm{PM}=180^\circ+\angle L(j\omega_{gc})\); \(\mathrm{GM}=-20\log|L(j\omega_{pc})|\); \(\zeta\approx\mathrm{PM}/100\)
Follower with GBW and second pole \(f_2\): \(\mathrm{PM}=90^\circ-\tan^{-1}(\text{GBW}/f_2)\)
Two-stage Miller op-amp: \(p_1\approx-1/(g_{m2}R_1R_2C_c)\), \(p_2\approx-g_{m2}/(C_1+C_2)\), \(\omega_t=g_{m1}/C_c\), \(z=g_{m2}/C_c\)
