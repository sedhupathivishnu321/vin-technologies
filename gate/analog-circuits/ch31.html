How this chapter is built

The problems below are original, pattern-based questions: for each topic they follow the type, difficulty and typical numbers of questions that GATE has repeatedly asked. They are not reproductions of specific past papers. Each topic gives three or four problems in increasing difficulty (1-mark MCQ style, 1-mark NAT, 2-mark numerical, 2-mark multi-step). Answers are at the end of each section.

31.1 Diodes

GATE-Pattern Problem

1. A Si diode conducts \(1\) mA at \(0.70\) V (\(n=1\), \(V_T=26\) mV). The current at \(0.76\) V is closest to (A) 2 mA (B) 5 mA (C) 10 mA (D) 20 mA.

2. Two identical diodes in parallel carry \(2\) mA total, each at \(V_D=0.70\) V. One is removed. The new \(V_D\) (V) is .

3. \(V_S=5\) V, \(R=1\) k\(\Omega\), series Si diode (\(0.7\) V). \(I=\)  mA.

4. \(C_{j0}=4\) pF, \(V_0=0.8\) V, \(m=0.5\). At \(V_R=3.2\) V the junction capacitance is  pF.

Answers: 1. \(e^{0.06/0.026}=10\) (C). 2. \(0.70+0.026\ln2=0.718\) V. 3. \(4.3\) mA. 4. \(4/\sqrt{1+4}=1.79\) pF.
