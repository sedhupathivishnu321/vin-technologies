33.1 Level 1 — Fundamental

GATE-Pattern Problem

1. Under reverse bias the depletion width of a PN junction (A) increases (B) decreases (C) is unchanged (D) vanishes.

2. The virtual-short property of an ideal op-amp with negative feedback means (A) \(v_+=v_-\) (B) \(v_o=0\) (C) \(i_o=0\) (D) \(A=0\).

3. A BJT is in the forward-active region when (A) both junctions forward (B) BE forward, BC reverse (C) both reverse (D) BE reverse, BC forward.

4. PIV of each diode of a bridge rectifier fed with peak \(V_m\) is (A) \(V_m\) (B) \(2V_m\) (C) \(V_m/2\) (D) \(V_m/\pi\).

5. Inverting amplifier, \(R_1=1\) k, \(R_f=10\) k. Gain (A) \(+10\) (B) \(-10\) (C) \(-11\) (D) \(-0.1\).

6. The Barkhausen condition for sustained oscillation is (A) \(A\beta=1\angle0^\circ\) (B) \(A\beta=0\) (C) \(A\beta=-1\angle0^\circ\) for positive feedback (D) \(A=\beta\).

7. An NMOS is in saturation when (A) \(V_{DS}\ge V_{GS}-V_{th}\) (B) \(V_{DS}<V_{GS}-V_{th}\) (C) \(V_{GS}<V_{th}\) (D) \(V_{DS}=0\).

8. A voltage gain of 100 is (A) 20 dB (B) 40 dB (C) 100 dB (D) 10 dB.

9. Negative feedback (A) desensitises the gain (B) increases distortion (C) reduces bandwidth (D) makes gain infinite.

10. The Colpitts oscillator uses (A) a capacitive divider (B) a tapped inductor (C) a crystal (D) an RC ladder.

11. The voltage gain of a voltage follower is (A) 0 (B) 1 (C) \(\infty\) (D) \(-1\).

12. An ideal integrator with a square-wave input produces (A) triangle (B) sine (C) square (D) impulses.

Answers: 1 A; 2 A; 3 B; 4 A; 5 B; 6 A; 7 A; 8 B; 9 A; 10 A; 11 B; 12 A.

33.3 Level 3 — Advanced

GATE-Pattern Problem

1. MOS divider bias: \(V_{DD}=10\) V, \(R_1=200\) k, \(R_2=100\) k, \(R_S=1\) k, \(R_D=2\) k, \(V_{th}=1\) V, \(k_n=2\) mA/V\({}^2\). Find \(I_D\) and \(V_{DS}\).

2. Two-stage CE amplifier: \(g_{m1}=g_{m2}=40\) mS, \(R_{C1}=4\) k, \(r_{\pi2}=2.5\) k, \(R_{C2}=R_L=4\) k. Overall gain in dB.

3. Widlar source for \(I_O=10\ \mu\)A from \(I_{REF}=1\) mA: \(R_E\).

4. MOS pair with mirror load: \(g_m=0.447\) mS, \(r_{o2}=r_{o4}=100\) k\(\Omega\): \(A_d\).

5. Order of a Butterworth low-pass: \(A_p=3\) dB at \(1\) kHz, \(A_s=40\) dB at \(4\) kHz.

6. Op-amp GBW \(=10\) MHz, inverting gain \(-49\): \(f_{-3dB}\).

7. Crystal with \(L_s=2\) H, \(C_s=0.01267\) pF, \(C_0=3\) pF: \(f_s\) and \(f_p\).

8. \(L(s)=100/[(1+s/10^3)(1+s/10^5)(1+s/10^6)]\): phase margin.

Answers: 1: \(I_D=1.226\) mA, \(V_{DS}=6.32\) V. 2: \(A_{v1}=-61.5\), \(A_{v2}=-80\): \(4923\to73.8\) dB. 3: \(12\) k\(\Omega\). 4: \(22\). 5: \(4\). 6: \(200\) kHz. 7: \(1.000\) MHz, \(1.0021\) MHz. 8: \(48.1^\circ\).

33.5 Multiple Select Questions

GATE-Pattern Problem

1. For a forward-biased Si diode as temperature rises at constant current: (A) \(V_D\) decreases (B) \(I_s\) increases (C) reverse leakage increases (D) \(r_d\) decreases.

2. Voltage-series negative feedback: (A) \(R_{in}\) increases (B) \(R_{out}\) decreases (C) bandwidth increases (D) distortion increases.

3. Inverting stages: (A) CE (B) inverting op-amp (C) CS (D) emitter follower.

4. Ideal op-amp with negative feedback: (A) \(v_+=v_-\) (B) input currents zero (C) \(R_{out}=0\) (D) gain depends on \(A\).

5. A Schmitt trigger: (A) has hysteresis (B) uses positive feedback (C) output has two levels (D) is a linear amplifier.

6. BJT small-signal: (A) \(g_m\propto I_C\) (B) \(r_\pi\propto\beta/I_C\) (C) \(r_o\propto V_A/I_C\) (D) \(g_m\propto\beta\).

7. LC oscillators: (A) Colpitts (B) Hartley (C) Wien (D) RC phase-shift.

8. A stable feedback system (min-phase): (A) PM \(>0\) (B) GM \(>0\) dB (C) \(\angle L=-200^\circ\) at \(\omega_{gc}\) (D) Nyquist encircles \(-1\).

Answers: 1: A, B, C. 2: A, B, C. 3: A, B, C. 4: A, B, C. 5: A, B, C. 6: A, B, C. 7: A, B. 8: A, B.

33.6 Multiple Choice Questions

GATE-Pattern Problem

1. The dominant contributor to the lower cutoff of a CE amplifier is usually (A) \(C_1\) (B) \(C_2\) (C) \(C_E\) (D) \(C_\mu\).

2. Which stage has no Miller multiplication? (A) CE (B) CS (C) CB/CG (D) inverting op-amp.

3. The CMRR of a differential pair is improved by (A) raising \(R_{EE}\) (B) lowering \(R_{EE}\) (C) raising \(R_C\) (D) lowering \(\beta\).

4. A source follower’s gain is reduced by (A) body effect (B) channel-length modulation only (C) \(C_{gd}\) (D) none.

5. Integrator with \(RC=1\) ms and a \(1\) V step: output slope (A) \(-1\) V/ms (B) \(+1\) V/ms (C) \(-1\) V/s (D) 0.

6. The stability factor of fixed bias is (A) \(\beta+1\) (B) 1 (C) \(\beta\) (D) \(1/\beta\).

7. Chebyshev filters have (A) passband ripple (B) flat passband (C) constant delay (D) no zeros.

8. CMOS Wilson mirror requires (A) 3 transistors (B) 2 (C) 5 (D) 1.

9. A comparator with hysteresis (A) reduces chatter (B) increases bandwidth (C) is a low-pass (D) linear.

10. An op-amp used as a comparator has (A) no feedback (B) negative feedback (C) unity gain (D) virtual short.

Answers: 1 C; 2 C; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.

33.7 Conceptual traps

GATE-Pattern Problem

State true or false and give the reason.
1. \(r_d\) depends on the DC voltage across the diode. 2. \(g_m\) of a BJT depends on \(\beta\). 3. In a source follower with body effect the gain exceeds \(g_mR/(1+g_mR)\). 4. A clamper changes the peak-to-peak amplitude. 5. The virtual short holds in a Schmitt trigger. 6. The bridge rectifier PIV is \(2V_m\). 7. Series mixing decreases input resistance. 8. A pole in the RHP is stable.

Answers: 1 False: depends on \(I_D\). 2 False: \(g_m=I_C/V_T\). 3 False: reduced. 4 False: only shifts. 5 False: positive feedback, saturated output. 6 False: \(V_m\). 7 False: increases. 8 False: unstable.

33.8 Full-length Analog Circuits test

Time: 50 minutes; 30 marks (ten 1-mark and ten 2-mark questions).

Part A: 1 mark each

1. Depletion width of a forward-biased junction (A) decreases (B) increases (C) unchanged.

2. Emitter-follower output resistance is (A) low (B) high (C) \(R_C\) (D) \(\infty\).

3. CMRR of an ideal pair with ideal tail source is (A) 0 (B) 1 (C) \(\infty\) (D) \(g_m\).

4. Miller effect increases the input capacitance of (A) inverting amplifiers (B) followers only (C) CB only (D) none.

5. An ideal integrator introduces a phase (A) \(+90^\circ\) (B) \(-90^\circ\) (C) \(0\) (D) \(180^\circ\) relative to \(\sin\) (with inversion included).

6. Sustained oscillation requires \(A\beta=\) (A) \(1\angle0^\circ\) (B) \(0\) (C) \(-1\) (D) \(\infty\).

7. The ripple factor of an ideal full-wave rectifier is (A) 0.482 (B) 1.21 (C) 0.406 (D) 0.

8. The Wilson mirror has (A) 3 (B) 2 (C) 4 transistors.

9. Fourth-order Butterworth roll-off is (A) \(-80\) (B) \(-40\) (C) \(-20\) (D) \(-60\) dB/dec.

10. Series mixing feedback (A) raises \(R_{in}\) (B) lowers \(R_{in}\) (C) no change.

Part B: 2 marks each

11. Shunt clipper with \(3\) V battery, \(0.7\) V diode, \(v_i=10\sin\omega t\): maximum output (V).

12. Fixed bias \(V_{CC}=9\) V, \(R_B=300\) k, \(R_C=2\) k, \(\beta=90\): \(V_{CE}\) (V).

13. CE with unbypassed \(R_E=100\ \Omega\), \(g_m=80\) mS, \(R_C\|R_L=2\) k: \(A_v\).

14. MOS divider bias (\(V_{DD}=10\), \(R_1=200\)k, \(R_2=100\)k, \(R_S=1\)k, \(V_{th}=1\), \(k_n=2\) mA/V\({}^2\)): \(I_D\) (mA).

15. Widlar: \(I_{REF}=1\) mA, \(I_O=10\ \mu\)A: \(R_E\) (k\(\Omega\)).

16. BJT pair, \(g_m=19.2\) mS, \(R_{EE}=100\) k, single-ended: CMRR.

17. Summer \(R_f=10\)k, \(R_1=10\)k, \(R_2=5\)k, \(R_3=2\)k, inputs \(1\) V each: \(v_o\) (V).

18. Sallen–Key (equal \(R\), \(C\)) Butterworth needs gain \(K=\) ?

19. Wien oscillator, \(R=10\) k, \(C=16\) nF: \(f_0\) (Hz).

20. Follower, GBW \(=1\) MHz, second pole \(500\) kHz: PM (\({}^\circ\)).

Answers: Part A: 1 A; 2 A; 3 C; 4 A; 5 A (cosine leads sine); 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A. Part B: 11: \(3.7\). 12: \(I_C=2.49\) mA, \(V_{CE}=4.02\). 13: \(-17.8\). 14: \(1.226\). 15: \(12\). 16: \(1920\) (\(65.7\) dB). 17: \(-8\). 18: \(1.586\). 19: \(995\). 20: \(90-\tan^{-1}(2)=26.6^\circ\).
