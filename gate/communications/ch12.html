12.1 One-page formula sheet

Random processes and noise

WSS: \(m_X\) const, \(R_X(\tau)\); \(R_X(0)=\) power, \(R_X(\infty)=m^2\); \(S_X=\mathcal F\{R_X\}\); \(S_Y=|H|^2S_X\).
\(N_0=kT\), \(-174\) dBm/Hz; \(v_n^2=4kTRB\); \(F=1+T_e/T_0\); Friis \(F_1+(F_2-1)/G_1\); matched filter \(2E/N_0\).

Analog

AM: \(P_t=P_c(1+\mu^2/2)\), \(\eta=\mu^2/(2+\mu^2)\), \(B=2W\). SSB \(B=W\). Image \(f_s+2f_{IF}\).
FM: \(\beta=\Delta f/f_m\), \(B=2(\Delta f+f_m)\), power \(A_c^2/2\). FOM: DSB/SSB 1, AM \(\eta\), FM \(\tfrac32\beta^2\).

Digital

\(f_s\ge2W\); \(\Delta=2m_p/2^n\); \(\sigma_q^2=\Delta^2/12\); SQNR \(6.02n+1.76\) dB; \(R_b=nf_s\).
Nyquist \(B_{min}=R_s/2\); raised cosine \((1+\alpha)R_s/2\); \(R_s=R_b/\log_2M\).
BPSK/QPSK \(Q(\sqrt{2E_b/N_0})\); coherent FSK/ASK \(Q(\sqrt{E_b/N_0})\); DPSK \(\tfrac12e^{-E_b/N_0}\); NC-FSK \(\tfrac12e^{-E_b/2N_0}\).

Information and coding

\(H=-\sum p\log_2p\); \(I=H(X)+H(Y)-H(X,Y)\); BSC \(1-H_b(p)\); \(C=B\log_2(1+\text{SNR})\); \(E_b/N_0\ge\ln2\).
\(d_{min}\): detect \(d-1\), correct \(\lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor\); Hamming \((2^r-1,2^r-1-r)\); \(s=rH^T\); convolutional \(2^{K-1}\) states.
Links: \(L_{fs}=20\log d+20\log f-147.55\); \(f_d=v/\lambda\); \(B_c=1/5\sigma_\tau\); \(NA=\sqrt{n_1^2-n_2^2}\).

12.3 Mixed mock test

Instructions. 18 questions: 8 one-mark, 6 two-mark, 4 NAT. Time 30 minutes.

Section A: 1-mark

1. The maximum power efficiency of single-tone AM (\(\mu\le1\)) is (A) 25% (B) 33.3% (C) 50% (D) 100%.

2. The Nyquist rate of \(\cos4000\pi t\) is (A) 2 kHz (B) 4 kHz (C) 8 kHz (D) 1 kHz.

3. An FM wave with \(\Delta f=10\) kHz and \(f_m=2\) kHz has Carson bandwidth (A) 12 kHz (B) 24 kHz (C) 20 kHz (D) 4 kHz.

4. Ideal SQNR of an 8-bit PCM (full-scale sine) is approximately (A) 40 dB (B) 49.9 dB (C) 56 dB (D) 60 dB.

5. Entropy of 8 equiprobable symbols: (A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 8 bits.

6. The BER of coherent BPSK is (A) \(Q(\sqrt{E_b/N_0})\) (B) \(Q(\sqrt{2E_b/N_0})\) (C) \(\tfrac12e^{-E_b/N_0}\) (D) \(\tfrac12e^{-E_b/2N_0}\).

7. Voice PCM with \(f_s=8\) kHz and 8 bits has bit rate (A) 8 kbps (B) 32 kbps (C) 64 kbps (D) 128 kbps.

8. In a superheterodyne receiver (\(f_{IF}=455\) kHz) tuned to 1000 kHz the image is at (A) 1455 (B) 1910 (C) 545 (D) 2365 kHz.

Section B: 2-mark

9. A tone-modulated AM wave with \(\mu=0.6\) and carrier power 1000 W has sideband power (A) 180 W (B) 360 W (C) 600 W (D) 90 W.

10. BPSK at \(E_b/N_0=4\) dB: \(P_b\approx\) (A) 0.0125 (B) 0.0228 (C) 0.159 (D) \(10^{-4}\).

11. A 4 kHz channel with \(\text{SNR}=15\) (linear) has capacity (A) 8 kbps (B) 12 kbps (C) 16 kbps (D) 60 kbps.

12. Binary data at 128 kbps with raised-cosine pulses, \(\alpha=0.5\), needs bandwidth (A) 64 kHz (B) 96 kHz (C) 128 kHz (D) 192 kHz.

13. A rectangular pulse of amplitude 3 V and duration \(2\,\mu\)s in noise with \(N_0=10^{-9}\) W/Hz: matched-filter peak SNR (dB) is (A) 36.6 (B) 45.6 (C) 42.6 (D) 48.6.

14. A delta modulator (\(f_s=16\) kHz) tracks \(2\cos(2\pi500t)\) V. Minimum step to avoid slope overload: (A) 0.2 V (B) 0.39 V (C) 0.78 V (D) 1.57 V.

Section C: NAT

15. Figure of merit (dB, one decimal) of tone FM with \(\beta=3\): .

16. Free-space path loss (dB, two decimals) at 1 GHz over 10 km: .

17. Average Huffman length (bits) for \(\{0.5,0.25,0.125,0.125\}\): .

18. Coded bits for a 50-bit message, rate-1/2 \(K=3\) convolutional code with tail bits: .

Answers with brief working

1 (B). 2 (B): \(f=2\) kHz. 3 (B): \(2(10+2)\). 4 (B). 5 (C). 6 (B). 7 (C). 8 (B).
9 (A): \(P_{SB}=\mu^2P_c/2=0.18P_c\). 10 (A): \(Q(2.24)\). 11 (C): \(4000\log_216\). 12 (B): \(\tfrac12(1.5)(128\text{ k})=96\) kHz. 13 (B): \(E=9\times2\times10^{-6}=1.8\times10^{-5}\), \(2E/N_0=36\,000\) (\(45.6\) dB). 14 (B): \(2\pi(500)(2)/16\,000=0.393\).
15: FOM \(=1.5\times9=13.5\) (\(11.3\) dB). 16: \(92.45+20\log_{10}1+20\log_{10}10=112.45\). 17: \(1.75\) (dyadic, equals the entropy). 18: \(2(50+2)=104\).

12.4 Final-week checklist

  1. Practise reading \(\mu\), \(\beta\), \(\Delta f\) from a given \(s(t)\) in under 30 seconds.
  2. Memorise the BER formulas and the quick values of \(Q(x)\).
  3. Redo one problem each on PCM bit rate, DM slope overload and Nyquist/raised cosine bandwidth.
  4. Do three entropy/Huffman problems and one capacity problem.
  5. Encode and decode one (7,4) Hamming word and one convolutional example.
  6. Review link-budget arithmetic in dB and the fibre \(NA\) formula.
