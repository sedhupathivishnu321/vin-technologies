Preface: How to Use These Notes

chapterPreface: How to Use These Notes

Communications carries a large share of the GATE ECE paper and mixes theory with quick numerical work: bandwidth and power of modulated signals, bit-error probabilities with the \(Q\)-function, entropy and capacity, and coding. These notes cover every topic of the Communications section of the syllabus, including the basic wireless and optical link items.

Originality and conventions

All problems are original; none is copied from an official paper or from any question bank, and none carries a year. Every numerical answer was recomputed with a script. \(Q(x)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_x^\infty e^{-t^2/2}dt\), with \(Q(1)=0.1587\), \(Q(2)=0.0228\), \(Q(3)=1.35\times10^{-3}\), \(Q(4)=3.17\times10^{-5}\). Noise PSD of white noise is \(N_0/2\) (two-sided). Logarithms: dB \(=10\log_{10}\) of power ratios; \(\log_2\) for information in bits.

Suggested 3-week plan. Week 1: Ch. 1–5 (random processes to analog). Week 2: Ch. 6–8 (digital). Week 3: Ch. 9–12 (information theory, coding, links, mock).
