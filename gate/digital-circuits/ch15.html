15.1 One-page formula sheet

Number systems and codes

2’s complement: \(-b_{n-1}2^{n-1}+\sum b_i2^i\); range \(-2^{n-1}\ldots2^{n-1}-1\); \(V=C_{n-1}\oplus C_n\).
IEEE single: \((-1)^s1.f\times2^{e-127}\) (1/8/23); double bias 1023 (1/11/52).
Gray: \(G=B\oplus(B\gg1)\). Hamming: \(2^r\ge k+r+1\); detect \(d-1\), correct \(\lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor\).

Boolean and combinational

Consensus \(AB+\overline AC+BC=AB+\overline AC\); \(A+\overline AB=A+B\); \(2^{2^n}\) functions; \(2^{2^{n-1}}\) self-dual.
FA: \(S=A\oplus B\oplus C\), \(C_o=AB+C(A\oplus B)\). CLA: \(C_{i+1}=G_i+P_iC_i\).
MUX \(2^n{:}1\): \(Y=\sum m_jI_j\); tree \((2^m-1)/(2^k-1)\). Flash ADC: \(2^n-1\) comparators.

Sequential

SR \(Q^+=S+\overline RQ\); JK \(J\overline Q+\overline KQ\); T \(T\oplus Q\); D \(D\).
Ring \(n\), Johnson \(2n\), LFSR \(2^n-1\). Detector length \(L\): Moore \(L+1\), Mealy \(L\).
Setup: \(T\ge t_{cq}+t_{logic,max}+t_{su}-\delta\); hold: \(t_{cq}+t_{logic,min}\ge t_h+\delta\).

Technology and architecture

\(P=\alpha CV^2f\); \(f_{ring}=1/(2Nt_{pd})\); LSB \(=V_{FS}/2^n\); SQNR \(=6.02n+1.76\).
Memory chips \(=(N/N_c)(m/m_c)\); ROM bits \(2^nm\); PLA points \(2np+pm\).
Pipeline time \((k+n-1)T\); CPI \(=1+\)stalls; CPU time \(=\text{IC}\cdot\text{CPI}/f\).

15.3 Mixed mock test

Instructions. 18 questions: 8 one-mark, 6 two-mark, 4 NAT. Time 30 minutes. Answers follow.

Section A: 1-mark

1. The 8-bit two’s-complement representation of \(-100\): (A) 10011100 (B) 01100100 (C) 10011011 (D) 11100100.

2. Number of 2-input NAND gates needed for a 2:1 multiplexer (inverter built from a NAND): (A) 3 (B) 4 (C) 5 (D) 6.

3. Minimum flip-flops for a MOD-100 counter: (A) 6 (B) 7 (C) 10 (D) 100.

4. The ADC with a conversion time independent of the input and equal to \(n\) clock cycles: (A) flash (B) counter (C) SAR (D) dual-slope.

5. Parity bits in a (15,11) Hamming code: (A) 3 (B) 4 (C) 5 (D) 11.

6. Number of states of a 6-flip-flop Johnson counter: (A) 6 (B) 12 (C) 36 (D) 64.

7. Number of Boolean functions of 2 variables: (A) 4 (B) 8 (C) 16 (D) 32.

8. A memory with 12 address lines and 8 data lines has capacity: (A) 4 Kbit (B) 8 Kbit (C) 32 Kbit (D) 96 Kbit.

Section B: 2-mark

9. \(F=\sum m(0,1,4,5,10,11,14,15)\) of \((A,B,C,D)\) equals (A) \(A\oplus C\) (B) \(A\odot C\) (C) \(B\odot D\) (D) \(AC\).

10. A 16-bit ripple-carry adder: each FA has carry delay 8 ns and carry-to-sum 10 ns. Delay of the last sum bit: (A) 128 (B) 130 (C) 138 (D) 160 ns.

11. \(t_{cq}=0.45\), \(t_{su}=0.25\), longest logic 1.8 (ns). Maximum frequency: (A) 400 MHz (B) 444 MHz (C) 500 MHz (D) 555 MHz.

12. A 4-stage pipeline executes 50 instructions. Speed-up over non-pipelined: (A) 4.00 (B) 3.77 (C) 3.5 (D) 3.0.

13. Number of states in the minimal Mealy and Moore machines for detecting the overlapping sequence 11: (A) 2 and 3 (B) 3 and 2 (C) 2 and 2 (D) 3 and 4.

14. Supply voltage and clock frequency are both halved. Dynamic power becomes (A) 1/2 (B) 1/4 (C) 1/8 (D) 1/16.

Section C: NAT

15. Number of minterms of \(F=\overline AB+\overline CD\) in four variables: .

16. Ideal SQNR of an 8-bit ADC (dB, one decimal): .

17. Transistors in a static CMOS gate \(Y=\overline{AB+CDE}\): .

18. Minimum number of 4-input LUTs for a 16-input AND: .

Answers with brief working

1 (A): \(100=01100100\), invert, \(+1\) gives \(10011100\). 2 (B): \(\overline S\) (1), \(\overline{\overline SA}\) (1), \(\overline{SB}\) (1), output NAND (1). 3 (B): \(2^7=128\). 4 (C). 5 (B). 6 (B): \(2n\). 7 (C). 8 (C): \(2^{12}\times8=32\) Kbit.
9 (B): the 1s are cells where \(A=C\); minimal SOP \(\overline A\,\overline C+AC\). 10 (B): \(15\times8+10\). 11 (A): \(T=2.5\) ns. 12 (B): \(200/53\). 13 (A): Mealy 2, Moore 3. 14 (C): \(P\propto V^2f\), so \(\tfrac14\cdot\tfrac12\).
15: \(4+4-1=7\). 16: \(6.02\times8+1.76=49.9\). 17: 5 literals \(\Rightarrow10\). 18: \(\lceil15/3\rceil=5\).

15.4 Final-week checklist

  1. Re-derive two’s-complement overflow and IEEE-754 decoding once on paper.
  2. Do five K-map problems with don’t-cares and count PIs/EPIs.
  3. Write all flip-flop characteristic and excitation tables from memory.
  4. For each counter type, state modulus and flip-flop count in one line.
  5. Practise two timing problems: setup (frequency) and hold (delay to insert).
  6. Revise CMOS PDN/PUN construction and transistor counts.
  7. Memorise ADC type comparison and memory/PLD arithmetic.
  8. Revisit pipeline speed-up and CPI formulas.
