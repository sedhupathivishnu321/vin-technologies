Preface: How to Use These Notes

chapterPreface: How to Use These Notes

Digital Circuits is among the most scoring subjects in GATE ECE: questions are short, deterministic and checkable. These notes cover every topic of the Digital Circuits section of the GATE ECE syllabus, plus the Computer Organization items that the official syllabus lists under the same heading (instruction set, addressing modes, ALU, data path, pipelining).

Boxes: blue = formulae, orange = solved example, green = shortcut, red = trap, violet/indigo/teal/magenta = problem types, yellow = test and revision.

Originality of the problems

All problems are original, written in the style and difficulty of GATE ECE. None is copied from an official paper and none carries a year or question number. Every numerical answer was recomputed independently. For official questions, download the papers from the GATE organising institute and use these notes as a toolkit. Content outside the syllabus core (a few computer-organization details) is labelled supplementary.

Conventions. \(\overline{A}\) or \(A'\) is NOT; \(+\) is OR; juxtaposition is AND; \(\oplus\) is XOR; \(\odot\) is XNOR. Minterms are \(m_i\), maxterms \(M_i\); \(\sum m(\dots)\) and \(\prod M(\dots)\) list them. \(Q^+\) (or \(Q_{n+1}\)) is the next state. \(\tpd\) is propagation delay, \(\tcq\) clock-to-Q delay, \(\tsu\) setup time and \(\thold\) hold time.

Suggested 3-week plan. Week 1: Ch. 1–6 (combinational). Week 2: Ch. 7–9 and 11 (sequential and timing). Week 3: Ch. 10, 12–15 (CMOS, memory, converters, architecture, revision).
