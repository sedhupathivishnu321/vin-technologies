How to use this chapter

Attempt the mixed set under exam conditions (about 2 minutes per mark). Then use the formula sheet and the checklist in the last two days before the exam. Transmission lines, waveguides and plane waves repay the most revision.

10.1 Mixed 1-mark problems

GATE 1-Mark Problems

Q1. The speed of an EM wave in a medium with \(\eps_r=9\), \(\mu_r=1\) is (A) \(c\) (B) \(c/3\) (C) \(c/9\) (D) \(3c\).

Q2. The displacement current density is (A) \(\vJ\) (B) \(\partial\vD/\partial t\) (C) \(\grad\times\vH\) (D) \(\sigma\vE\).

Q3. The VSWR on a line terminated in a short circuit is (A) 0 (B) 1 (C) \(\infty\) (D) \(-1\).

Q4. At the cutoff frequency of a waveguide mode the guide wavelength \(\lambda_g\) is (A) 0 (B) \(\lambda_0\) (C) \(\infty\) (D) \(2\lambda_0\).

Q5. The radiation resistance of a \(\lambda/4\) monopole over a perfect ground is (A) 36.5 \(\Omega\) (B) 73 \(\Omega\) (C) 146 \(\Omega\) (D) 377 \(\Omega\).

Q6. The divergence theorem converts a closed-surface integral of \(\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf S\) into (A) \(\oint\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf l\) (B) \(\iiint\grad\cdot\mathbf A\,dV\) (C) \(\iiint\grad\times\mathbf A\,dV\) (D) \(\iint\grad\mathbf A\,dS\).

Answers: Q1 (B). Q2 (B). Q3 (C). Q4 (C): \(\beta=0\). Q5 (A). Q6 (B).

10.2 Mixed 2-mark problems

GATE 2-Mark Problems

Q7. A lossless line of \(Z_0=50\,\Omega\) is terminated in \(Z_L=25\,\Omega\). \(|\Gamma|\) and VSWR are (A) \(1/3\), 2 (B) \(1/2\), 3 (C) \(1/3\), 3 (D) \(1/2\), 2.

Q8. A plane wave \(E_0=754\) V/m travels in free space. Its average power density is (A) 377 W/m\({}^2\) (B) 754 W/m\({}^2\) (C) 1508 W/m\({}^2\) (D) 188 W/m\({}^2\).

Q9. A rectangular guide has \(a=4\,\)cm, \(b=2\,\)cm. The first three cutoff frequencies (GHz) are (A) 3.75, 7.5, 7.5 (B) 3.75, 7.5, 8.4 (C) 3.75, 5.3, 7.5 (D) 7.5, 7.5, 15.

Q10. A \(10\,\)GHz plane wave in free space meets a perfect conductor at normal incidence. The distance from the surface to the nearest \(E\)-field maximum is (A) \(0.75\,\)cm (B) \(1.5\,\)cm (C) \(3\,\)cm (D) \(0.375\,\)cm.

Q11. Two identical isotropic sources, spacing \(\lambda/2\), fed in phase. The array factor has nulls along (A) the broadside direction (B) the array axis (C) \(45^\circ\) (D) nowhere.

Answers: Q7 (A): \(\Gamma=\frac{25-50}{75}=-\frac13\), \(s=\frac{1+1/3}{1-1/3}=2\). Q8 (B): \(\frac{754^2}{2\cdot377}=754\) W/m\({}^2\). Q9 (A): TE\(_{10}\) \(3.75\), TE\(_{20}\) \(7.5\), TE\(_{01}\) \(7.5\) GHz (TE\(_{11}\) follows at \(8.38\)). Q10 (A): the \(E\) field is zero at the wall, so the first maximum is \(\lambda/4=0.75\,\)cm away (\(\lambda=3\,\)cm). Q11 (B): \(\mathrm{AF}=2\cos(\frac\pi2\cos\theta)\) vanishes at \(\theta=0,\pi\).

Numerical Answer Type (NAT)

Q12. A lossless line has \(Z_0=75\,\Omega\) and a load \(Z_L=150\,\Omega\). VSWR \(=\) .

Q13. Skin depth (\(\mu\)m) of copper at \(4\,\)MHz (using \(66\,\mu\)m at \(1\,\)MHz): .

Q14. Free-space path loss (dB, nearest integer) at \(1\,\)GHz over \(10\,\)km: .

Q15. Plates of area \(200\,\mathrm{cm^2}\), spacing \(2\,\)mm, \(\eps_r=1\): capacitance (pF, one decimal): .

Answers: Q12: \(\Gamma=\frac13\), \(s=2\). Q13: \(33\). Q14: \(112\) (112.45). Q15: \(8.854\times10^{-12}\cdot0.02/0.002=88.5\) pF. Q16: \(10\log1.64=2.15\) dBi.

10.4 Mixed MSQ problems

Multiple Select (MSQ)

Q17. For a lossless transmission line: (A) \(|\Gamma|\) is constant along the line (B) \(Z_{in}\) repeats every \(\lambda/2\) (C) \(Z_0\) depends on the line length (D) a quarter-wave section inverts impedance about \(Z_0^2\).

Q18. Which statements about a plane wave in a good conductor are true? (A) \(\alpha=\beta\) (B) skin depth decreases with frequency (C) \(\vE\) and \(\vH\) are in phase (D) \(\eta_c\) has a \(45^\circ\) angle.

Answers: Q17: (A), (B), (D). Q18: (A), (B), (D).

Maxwell\(\grad\times\vE=-\partial_t\vB\), \(\grad\times\vH=\vJ+\partial_t\vD\), \(\grad\cdot\vD=\rho_v\), \(\grad\cdot\vB=0\)

10.6 Topic checklist

  • Can write \(\grad\cdot\) and \(dV\) in cylindrical and spherical coordinates.
  • Can apply Gauss’s and Ampère’s laws to symmetric geometries and state boundary conditions.
  • Can recognise Maxwell’s equations in either form and compute the loss tangent.
  • Can compute \(\beta\), \(\eta\), \(\lambda\), \(\delta\) for a given medium.
  • Can find \(\Gamma\), VSWR, \(Z_{in}\) and a matching section for a transmission line, and read the Smith chart.
  • Can list waveguide modes below a given frequency and compute \(\lambda_g\), \(v_p\), \(v_g\).
  • Can use directivity, gain, \(A_e\) and the Friis equation, and sketch simple array patterns.
