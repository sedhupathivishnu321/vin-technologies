Preface: How to Use These Notes

chapterPreface: How to Use These Notes

Electromagnetics is the subject students fear most and the one with the most predictable question types: a reflection coefficient, a Smith-chart or stub-matching step, a waveguide cutoff, a skin depth, a Friis calculation. These notes cover every topic of the Electromagnetics section of the GATE ECE syllabus, in the order a first course teaches it, with the numerical shortcuts that make each topic a one-minute question.

Originality of the problems

All problems are original, written in the style and difficulty of GATE ECE. None is copied from an official paper and none carries a year or question number. Every numerical answer was recomputed independently by script. For official questions, download the papers from the GATE organising institute and use these notes as a toolkit.

Constants and conventions. \(\epsz=8.854\times10^{-12}\,\)F/m, \(\muz=4\pi\times10^{-7}\,\)H/m, \(c=1/\sqrt{\muz\epsz}=3\times10^8\,\)m/s, \(\eta_0=\sqrt{\muz/\epsz}\approx120\pi\approx377\,\Omega\), \(1/(4\pi\epsz)=9\times10^9\). Phasors use \(e^{j\omega t}\). Bold letters are vectors, \(\ar_x\) etc. are unit vectors, \(\grad\) is the del operator. Unless stated, amplitudes are peak values.

Suggested 3-week plan. Week 1: Ch. 1–4 (statics, Maxwell). Week 2: Ch. 5–7 (waves, reflection, transmission lines). Week 3: Ch. 8–10 (waveguides, antennas, revision). Transmission lines and waveguides carry the most marks and deserve the most repetition.
