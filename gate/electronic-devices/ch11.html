11.1 One-page formula sheet

Semiconductor physics

\(np=n_i^2\); \(n=n_ie^{(E_F-E_i)/kT}\); \(n_i\propto T^{3/2}e^{-E_g/2kT}\); \(\lambda(\mu\text{m})=1.24/E_g\).
\(\sigma=q(n\mu_n+p\mu_p)\); \(J_n=qD_n\,dn/dx\); \(D/\mu=kT/q\); \(L=\sqrt{D\tau}\); \(\Delta p(t)=\Delta p_0e^{-t/\tau}\).
\(d\mathcal E/dx=\rho/\varepsilon\); \(\;V_T=0.0259\) V; one decade \(=59.5\) mV.

Diodes

\(V_{bi}=V_T\ln\dfrac{N_AN_D}{n_i^2}\), \(\ W=\sqrt{\dfrac{2\varepsilon(V_{bi}-V)}q\left(\dfrac1{N_A}+\dfrac1{N_D}\right)}\), \(\ C_j\propto(V_{bi}+V_R)^{-m}\).
\(I=I_s(e^{V/\eta V_T}-1)\); \(r_d=\eta V_T/I\); \(C_d=\tau I/\eta V_T\); \(-2\) mV/\({}^\circ\)C.
Solar: \(V_{oc}=V_T\ln(1+I_{ph}/I_s)\), \(FF=P_m/V_{oc}I_{sc}\). Photodiode \(R=\eta\lambda/1.24\). Zener \(I_Z=I_R-I_L\).

Transistors

BJT: \(\alpha=\beta/(\beta+1)\), \(I_{CEO}=(\beta+1)I_{CBO}\), \(I_C=I_Se^{V_{BE}/V_T}(1+V_{CE}/V_A)\), \(g_m=I_C/V_T\), \(r_\pi=\beta/g_m\), \(r_o=V_A/I_C\), \(f_T=g_m/2\pi(C_\pi+C_\mu)\).
MOS: \(V_T=V_{FB}+2\phi_F+|Q_d|/C_{ox}\); \(I_D=\tfrac12k'(W/L)V_{ov}^2(1+\lambda V_{DS})\); \(g_m=2I_D/V_{ov}\); \(\gamma(\sqrt{2\phi_F+V_{SB}}-\sqrt{2\phi_F})\).
JFET: \(I_D=I_{DSS}(1-V_{GS}/V_P)^2\).
Scaling \(S\): delay \(1/S\), power/gate \(1/S^2\), power density constant.

11.3 Mixed mock test

Instructions. 18 questions: 8 one-mark, 6 two-mark, 4 NAT. Time 30 minutes. Constants as in the preface.

Section A: 1-mark

1. Electron and hole concentrations in silicon doped with \(N_D=10^{15}\,\text{cm}^{-3}\): (A) \(10^{15}\), \(2.25\times10^{5}\) (B) \(10^{15}\), \(10^{15}\) (C) \(2.25\times10^{5}\), \(10^{15}\) (D) \(1.5\times10^{10}\) each.

2. The diffusion coefficient of electrons with \(\mu_n=1350\,\text{cm}^2/\text{V\,s}\) at 300 K is (A) 35 cm\({}^2\)/s (B) 1350 (C) 0.35 (D) 3.5.

3. Avalanche breakdown has a (A) negative temperature coefficient (B) positive temperature coefficient (C) zero (D) none.

4. A BJT is in the forward-active region when (A) EBJ forward, CBJ reverse (B) both forward (C) both reverse (D) EBJ reverse, CBJ forward.

5. An n-MOSFET is saturated for (A) \(V_{DS}<V_{GS}-V_T\) (B) \(V_{DS}\ge V_{GS}-V_T\) (C) \(V_{GS}<V_T\) (D) \(V_{DS}\ge V_T\).

6. A Schottky diode conducts by (A) minority carriers (B) majority carriers (C) tunnelling only (D) avalanche.

7. The short-circuit current of a solar cell is proportional to (A) \(\ln\)(light) (B) light intensity (C) temperature (D) \(V_{oc}\).

8. An efficient LED requires (A) indirect gap (B) direct gap (C) metal (D) insulator.

Section B: 2-mark

9. Built-in potential for \(N_A=N_D=10^{16}\,\text{cm}^{-3}\) in Si: (A) 0.59 V (B) 0.69 V (C) 0.79 V (D) 0.89 V.

10. An abrupt junction with \(V_{bi}=0.6\) V: \(C(0)/C(V_R=2.4\text{ V})\) is (A) 2 (B) \(\sqrt5\) (C) 5 (D) 4.

11. A BJT with \(\beta=100\) at \(I_C=0.5\) mA (\(V_T=25.9\) mV): \(g_m\) and \(r_\pi\) are (A) 19.3 mS, 5.18 k\(\Omega\) (B) 38.6 mS, 2.59 k\(\Omega\) (C) 19.3 mS, 2.59 k\(\Omega\) (D) 9.6 mS, 10.4 k\(\Omega\).

12. \(k_n'=200\,\mu\)A/V\({}^2\), \(W/L=5\), \(V_{ov}=0.4\) V, \(\lambda=0\), saturation. \(I_D\) is (A) 80 \(\mu\)A (B) 160 \(\mu\)A (C) 40 \(\mu\)A (D) 400 \(\mu\)A.

13. A Zener regulator: \(V_{in}=15\) V, \(R_s=470\,\Omega\), \(V_Z=9.1\) V, load current 5 mA. \(I_Z\) is (A) 12.6 mA (B) 7.6 mA (C) 5 mA (D) 2.6 mA.

14. Growing 0.5 \(\mu\)m of oxide consumes silicon of thickness (A) 0.22 \(\mu\)m (B) 0.5 \(\mu\)m (C) 0.44 \(\mu\)m (D) 1.1 \(\mu\)m.

Section C: NAT

15. \(E_F-E_i\) (eV, two decimals) for \(n=10^{17}\,\text{cm}^{-3}\) in Si (\(kT=0.0259\), \(n_i=1.5\times10^{10}\)): .

16. Wavelength (nm) of an LED with \(E_g=2.0\) eV: .

17. \(g_{m0}\) (mS) of a JFET with \(I_{DSS}=12\) mA and \(V_P=-3\) V: .

18. Poisson yield (%, one decimal) for \(A=1.5\,\text{cm}^2\), \(D_0=1\,\text{cm}^{-2}\): .

Answers with brief working

1 (A): \(p=2.25\times10^{20}/10^{15}\). 2 (A): \(0.0259\times1350=35\). 3 (B). 4 (A). 5 (B). 6 (B). 7 (B). 8 (B).
9 (B): \(0.0259\ln(10^{32}/2.25\times10^{20})=0.695\). 10 (B): \(\sqrt{(0.6+2.4)/0.6}=\sqrt5\). 11 (A): \(g_m=0.5/25.9=19.3\) mS, \(r_\pi=100/19.3\,\text{mS}=5.18\) k\(\Omega\). 12 (A): \(\tfrac12(200)(5)(0.16)=80\,\mu\)A. 13 (B): \(I_R=5.9/470=12.55\) mA, \(I_Z=7.55\) mA. 14 (A): \(0.44\times0.5\).
15: \(0.0259\ln(6.67\times10^6)=0.0259\times15.71=0.41\). 16: \(1240/2=620\). 17: \(2\times12/3=8\). 18: \(e^{-1.5}=22.3\).

11.4 Final-week checklist

  1. Redo carrier-concentration and \(E_F\) problems until you can do them in 1 minute.
  2. Memorise junction formulae (\(V_{bi}\), \(W\), \(x_n/x_p\), \(C_j\)) and their scaling.
  3. Practise Zener, solar-cell and photodiode calculations once each.
  4. BJT: two bias problems (fixed, divider) and one small-signal parameter problem.
  5. MOS: \(V_T\) with body effect, triode and saturation current, \(g_m\) and \(r_o\).
  6. Fabrication: oxide thickness, Gaussian diffusion/implant, lithography formula.
