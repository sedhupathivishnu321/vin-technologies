Preface: How to Use These Notes

chapterPreface: How to Use These Notes

Electronic Devices is a compact, formula-driven GATE ECE subject: carrier statistics, junction electrostatics, diode and transistor equations, and device physics of MOS structures. These notes cover every topic of the Electronic Devices part of the syllabus and the device-fabrication items that GATE lists with it.

Originality and constants

All problems are original; none is copied from an official paper and none carries a year. Every numerical answer was recomputed with a script. Constants used (state them in the exam when not given): \(T=300\,\)K, \(kT/q=0.0259\,\)V, \(n_i(\text{Si})=1.5\times10^{10}\,\text{cm}^{-3}\), \(E_g(\text{Si})=1.12\,\)eV, \(\varepsilon_{Si}=11.7\varepsilon_0\), \(\varepsilon_{ox}=3.9\varepsilon_0\), \(\varepsilon_0=8.854\times10^{-14}\,\)F/cm, \(q=1.6\times10^{-19}\,\)C, \(\mu_n=1350\), \(\mu_p=480\ \text{cm}^2/\text{V\,s}\). Official papers sometimes give slightly different values (\(n_i=1.45\) or \(1\times10^{10}\)): always use what the question gives.

Suggested 2-week plan. Week 1: Ch. 1–5. Week 2: Ch. 6–11.
