8.1 Mixed 1-mark problems

GATE 1-Mark Problems

Q1. A \(2\times2\) matrix has trace 5 and determinant 6. Its eigenvalues are (A) 1, 6 (B) 2, 3 (C) \(-2,-3\) (D) 5, 1.

Q2. \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\tan x}{x}=\) (A) 0 (B) 1 (C) \(\infty\) (D) \(\frac12\).

Q3. For \(y''-5y'+6y=0\) the roots of the auxiliary equation are (A) \(2,3\) (B) \(-2,-3\) (C) \(1,6\) (D) \(\pm\sqrt6\).

Q4. \(\grad\times\mathbf r=\) (A) 3 (B) \(\mathbf0\) (C) \(\mathbf r\) (D) 1.

Q5. The mean of the exponential distribution with \(\lambda=0.5\) is (A) 0.5 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 4.

Q6. \(\oint_{|z|=1}z^2dz=\) (A) \(2\pi j\) (B) \(0\) (C) \(\pi\) (D) \(j\).

Q7. Which numerical rule needs an even number of intervals? (A) Trapezoidal (B) Simpson 1/3 (C) Euler (D) Newton–Raphson.

Answers: Q1 (B). Q2 (B). Q3 (A). Q4 (B). Q5 (C). Q6 (B), \(z^2\) is entire. Q7 (B).

8.2 Mixed 2-mark problems

GATE 2-Mark Problems

Q8. \(A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\). Then \(A^{10}=\) (A) \(\begin{bmatrix}1&10\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\) (B) \(\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&10\end{bmatrix}\) (C) \(\begin{bmatrix}10&10\\0&10\end{bmatrix}\) (D) \(I\).

Q9. \(\int_0^\infty x^2e^{-2x}dx=\) (A) \(\frac14\) (B) \(\frac12\) (C) \(\frac18\) (D) \(2\).

Q10. \(y'+y=e^{-x}\), \(y(0)=0\). Then \(y(1)=\) (A) \(e^{-1}\) (B) \(1-e^{-1}\) (C) \(2e^{-1}\) (D) \(0\).

Q11. Two fair coins and one fair die are thrown. \(P(\text{2 heads and an even number})=\) (A) \(\frac18\) (B) \(\frac14\) (C) \(\frac1{12}\) (D) \(\frac16\).

Q12. \(\Res\big(\frac{e^z}{z^2},0\big)=\) (A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) \(\frac12\).

Answers: Q8 (A): \(A^n=\begin{bmatrix}1&n\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\). Q9 (A): \(\Gamma(3)/2^3=2/8=\frac14\). Q10 (A): \(\mu=e^x\), \((e^xy)'=1\), \(y=xe^{-x}\), \(y(1)=e^{-1}\). Q11 (A): \(\frac14\cdot\frac12=\frac18\). Q12 (B): \(e^z/z^2=z^{-2}+z^{-1}+\cdots\).

8.3 Mixed NAT problems

Numerical Answer Type (NAT)

Q13. Eigenvalues of \(\begin{bmatrix}3&0&0\\1&2&0\\4&5&7\end{bmatrix}\) sum to .

Q14. The minimum of \(f(x,y)=x^2+y^2-2x-4y+9\) is .

Q15. \(X\sim N(10,4)\). \(P(X>10)=\) .

Q16. Flux of \(\mathbf F=(x,y,z)\) outward through the unit cube \([0,1]^3\) is .

Q17. Trapezoidal rule with \(h=0.5\) for \(\int_0^1x\,dx\) is .

8.4 Mixed MSQ problems

Multiple Select (MSQ)

Q18. For a \(3\times3\) real matrix with \(\det A=0\): (A) \(0\) is an eigenvalue (B) \(A\) is invertible (C) \(\rank A<3\) (D) \(Ax=0\) has a non-trivial solution.

Q19. Which of these are valid tests of series convergence? (A) Ratio test (B) \(p\)-series test (C) Cauchy–Riemann test (D) Alternating-series test.

Answers: Q18: (A), (C), (D). Q19: (A), (B), (D).

8.6 Topic checklist

  • Can find eigenvalues of \(2\times2\) and \(3\times3\) matrices and apply Cayley–Hamilton.
  • Can classify a stationary point with the second-derivative test.
  • Can solve first-order linear, separable and constant-coefficient second-order ODEs, including resonance.
  • Can use \(\grad\), \(\grad\cdot\), \(\grad\times\) and choose among Gauss, Stokes and Green.
  • Can apply Bayes and use the table of standard distributions.
  • Can compute residues at simple and higher-order poles.
  • Can do two iterations of Newton, trapezoid and Simpson by hand.
