Preface: How to Use These Notes

chapterPreface: How to Use These Notes

Engineering Mathematics carries about 13 marks in GATE ECE and rewards speed: most questions are one-step identities (trace and determinant of eigenvalues, a Taylor coefficient, a residue, a standard distribution moment). These notes cover every topic of the Engineering Mathematics section of the GATE ECE syllabus, with the short-cut techniques that save minutes in the exam.

Anatomy of a chapter. Core concept \(\to\) standard results \(\to\) derivation or construction \(\to\) solved examples \(\to\) traps \(\to\) shortcuts \(\to\) 1-mark, 2-mark, NAT and MSQ problems with answers \(\to\) chapter test \(\to\) one-page revision. Boxes: blue = formulae, orange = solved example, green = shortcut, red = trap, violet/indigo/teal/magenta = problem types, yellow = test and revision.

Originality of the problems

All problems are original, written in the style and difficulty of GATE ECE. None is copied from an official paper and none carries a year or question number. Every numerical answer was recomputed independently by script. For official questions, download the papers from the GATE organising institute and use these notes as a toolkit.

Conventions. \(A^T\) is the transpose; \(\lambda\) denotes eigenvalues; \(\E[\cdot]\) and \(\Var(\cdot)\) are expectation and variance; \(\Res(f,a)\) is a residue; \(\grad\) is the del operator; \(j=\sqrt{-1}\). Angles are in radians unless marked.

Suggested 2-week plan. Week 1: Ch. 1–4 (algebra, calculus, ODE, vectors). Week 2: Ch. 5–7 and the revision chapter. Spend the last two days on Ch. 8 and the formula boxes.
