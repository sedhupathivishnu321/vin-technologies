11.1 Mixed 1-mark problems

GATE 1-Mark Problems

Q1. “The committee divided in its opinion.” Choose the best word: (A) were (B) are (C) was (D) have.

Q2. \(20\%\) of \(\frac13\) of \(150\) is (A) 5 (B) 10 (C) 15 (D) 30.

Q3. If \(\log_2x=5\) then \(x=\) (A) 10 (B) 25 (C) 32 (D) 64.

Q4. A cube of side \(4\) has surface area (A) 64 (B) 96 (C) 24 (D) 16.

Q5. Next in \(5,11,23,47,\dots\) (A) 94 (B) 95 (C) 96 (D) 100.

Q6. A train \(200\,\)m long passes a pole in \(10\) s. Its speed in km/h is (A) 20 (B) 36 (C) 72 (D) 90.

Answers: Q1 (C): the committee acts as one unit. Q2 (B): \(\frac13\cdot150=50\), \(20\%\) is \(10\). Q3 (C). Q4 (B): \(6\times16\). Q5 (B): \(x\to2x+1\). Q6 (C): \(20\) m/s.

11.2 Mixed 2-mark problems

GATE 2-Mark Problems

Q7. The price of an item rises by \(25\%\) and then falls by \(20\%\). The net change is (A) \(0\%\) (B) \(5\%\) rise (C) \(5\%\) fall (D) \(10\%\) rise.

Q8. Two persons, \(A\) and \(B\), finish a job in \(12\) and \(24\) days alone. They work together for \(4\) days, then \(A\) leaves. \(B\) finishes the rest in (A) 8 days (B) 12 days (C) 10 days (D) 16 days.

Q9. Four distinct digits are chosen from \(1\)–\(9\) to form \(4\)-digit numbers with no repeats. The count is (A) 3024 (B) 6561 (C) 9! (D) 126.

Q10. A box has \(3\) red and \(2\) blue balls. Two are drawn without replacement. The probability both are red is (A) \(3/10\) (B) \(9/25\) (C) \(3/5\) (D) \(2/5\).

Q11. Statements: “All metals conduct; some solids are metals.” Which must be true? (A) All solids conduct (B) Some solids conduct (C) No solid conducts (D) All conductors are metals.

Answers: Q7 (A): \(1.25\times0.8=1\). Q8 (B): together \(\frac1{12}+\frac1{24}=\frac18\) per day, so \(4\) days finish \(\frac12\); \(B\) does the remaining \(\frac12\) at \(\frac1{24}\) per day, taking \(12\) days. Q9 (A): \({}^9P_4=9\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6=3024\). Q10 (A): \(\frac35\cdot\frac24=\frac3{10}\). Q11 (B).

11.3 Mixed NAT problems

Numerical Answer Type (NAT)

Q12. A rectangle has length \(12\) and breadth \(5\). Its diagonal is .

Q13. The average of \(5\) numbers is \(20\). Adding a sixth number makes the average \(22\). The sixth number is .

Q14. The number of trailing zeros of \(50!\) is .

Q15. A boat with still-water speed \(10\) km/h goes \(24\) km downstream in \(2\) h. The stream speed (km/h) is .

Q16. The number of lines of symmetry of a regular octagon is .

Answers: Q12: \(13\). Q13: \(6\times22-5\times20=32\). Q14: \(10+2=12\). Q15: downstream speed \(12\), stream \(2\). Q16: \(8\).

11.4 Mixed MSQ problems

Multiple Select (MSQ)

Q17. Which are grammatically correct? (A) Each of the players has a bat. (B) Neither of the answers are correct. (C) She is senior to him. (D) The news is encouraging.

Q18. For a pie chart which hold? (A) The central angle of \(25\%\) is \(90^\circ\). (B) The values can be read directly without the total. (C) Percentages sum to \(100\). (D) A segment of \(12\%\) of \(45\) crore is \(5.4\) crore.

Answers: Q17: (A), (C), (D). Q18: (A), (C), (D).

11.6 Exam strategy for GA

  • Take GA first or last consistently; target 12–15 minutes in total.
  • Do verbal in under \(45\) seconds each; skip a long passage if it costs more than \(2\) minutes.
  • For quant, write the setup (rate, ratio, total) before computing; verify with a quick estimate.
  • Never leave an MCQ blank (negative marking is \(\frac13\) for one-mark and \(\frac23\) for two-mark MCQs; there is none for NAT or MSQ). Guess only after eliminating at least two options.
