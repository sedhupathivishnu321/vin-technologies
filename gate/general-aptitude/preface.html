Preface: How to Use These Notes

chapterPreface: How to Use These Notes

General Aptitude (GA) carries 15 marks in every GATE paper (5 one-mark and 5 two-mark questions) and is the cheapest score in the exam: the questions need no engineering knowledge, and practice converts them into near-certain marks. These notes cover every topic of the General Aptitude section of the GATE ECE syllabus.

Anatomy of a chapter. Core concept \(\to\) standard results \(\to\) solved examples \(\to\) traps \(\to\) shortcuts \(\to\) 1-mark, 2-mark, NAT and MSQ problems with answers \(\to\) chapter test \(\to\) one-page revision. Boxes: blue = formulae, orange = solved example, green = shortcut, red = trap, violet/indigo/teal/magenta = problem types, yellow = test and revision.

Originality of the problems

All problems are original, written in the style and difficulty of GATE GA. None is copied from an official paper and none carries a year or question number. Every numerical answer was recomputed independently by script. Reading passages are original short texts written for practice. For official questions, download the papers from the GATE organising institute and use these notes as a toolkit.

Conventions. Options are labelled (A)–(D). A NAT question has a numerical answer typed in; an MSQ question may have more than one correct option. Percentages are written with \(\%\); \(\log\) without a base is base 10 unless stated. All monetary examples are in rupees.

Suggested 10-day plan. Days 1–2: verbal (Ch. 1–2) plus 10 minutes of reading daily. Days 3–7: quantitative chapters (Ch. 3–8), one per day with 20 timed problems. Days 8–9: reasoning and spatial (Ch. 9–10). Day 10: Ch. 11 under exam timing. Aim for about 90 seconds per one-mark and 2 minutes per two-mark question.
