Trigonometry and hyperbolic functions

1.4

\(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\)\(\sin2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta\); \(\cos2\theta=\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta\)
\(\cos^2\theta=\frac{1+\cos2\theta}2\); \(\sin^2\theta=\frac{1-\cos2\theta}2\)\(\sin A\cos B=\frac12[\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B)]\)
\(\cos A\cos B=\frac12[\cos(A-B)+\cos(A+B)]\)\(\sin A\sin B=\frac12[\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)]\)
\(\sin(\theta\pm90^\circ)=\pm\cos\theta\); \(\cos(\theta\pm90^\circ)=\mp\sin\theta\)\(\sinh x=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2\), \(\cosh x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2\)
\(\cosh^{-1}x=\ln\left(x+\sqrt{x^2-1}\right)\)\(\tan\frac\Omega2\) (bilinear warping)

Series and sums

  • Geometric: \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}r^n=\frac1{1-r}\) (\(|r|<1\)); \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}r^n=\frac{1-r^N}{1-r}\); \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty nr^n=\frac r{(1-r)^2}\).
  • \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^Nn=\frac{N(N+1)}2\); \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^Nn^2=\frac{N(N+1)(2N+1)}6\); \(\displaystyle\sum_{n\ \mathrm{odd}}\frac1{n^2}=\frac{\pi^2}8\).
  • Exponential: \(e^x=\sum x^n/n!\). Small angles: \(\sin x\approx x\), \(\cos x\approx1-x^2/2\).

Calculus

  • \(\int e^{at}dt=\frac1ae^{at}\); \(\int te^{at}dt=\frac{e^{at}}a\left(t-\frac1a\right)\); \(\int_0^\infty e^{-at}dt=\frac1a\); \(\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{dx}{1+x^2}=\pi\).
  • Integration by parts \(\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du\). Leibniz rule for \(\frac{d}{dt}\int_0^tf(t,\tau)d\tau\).
  • L’Hôpital: \(\lim\frac{f}{g}=\lim\frac{f'}{g'}\) for \(0/0\), \(\infty/\infty\).
  • \(\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin x}xdx=\frac\pi2\); \(\int_{-\infty}^\infty\sinc^2(x)dx=1\).

Partial fractions

Distinct real: \(\frac{N(s)}{\prod(s+p_k)}=\sum\frac{A_k}{s+p_k}\), \(A_k=[(s+p_k)F]_{s=-p_k}\). Repeated \((s+p)^2\): \(\frac{A_1}{s+p}+\frac{A_2}{(s+p)^2}\), \(A_2=[(s+p)^2F]_{-p}\), \(A_1=\frac d{ds}[(s+p)^2F]_{-p}\). Improper: divide first. Complex pair: complete the square.

Linear algebra

\(2\times2\) inverse: \(\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}^{-1}=\frac1{ad-bc}\begin{bmatrix}d&-b\\-c&a\end{bmatrix}\). Cramer’s rule: \(x_k=\det\mathbf A_k/\det\mathbf A\). Eigenvalues: \(\det(\mathbf A-\lambda\mathbf I)=0\).
