Differential Equations

First order: \(\dfrac{dx}{dt}+\dfrac x\tau=f(t)\)

Homogeneous solution \(Ke^{-t/\tau}\); particular solution by inspection. For DC input \(x_p=\tau f\); complete solution \(x=x_\infty+(x(0)-x_\infty)e^{-t/\tau}\).

Second order: \(\ddot x+2\alpha\dot x+\omega_0^2x=f\)

RootsConditionHomogeneous solution
real, distinct\(\alpha>\omega_0\)\(A_1e^{s_1t}+A_2e^{s_2t}\)
real, equal\(\alpha=\omega_0\)\((A_1+A_2t)e^{-\alpha t}\)
complex \(-\alpha\pm j\omega_d\)\(\alpha<\omega_0\)\(e^{-\alpha t}(B_1\cos\omega_dt+B_2\sin\omega_dt)\)

Particular solutions (undetermined coefficients)

Forcing \(f(t)\)Try \(x_p\)
constant \(K\)\(A\)
\(Kt\)\(At+B\)
\(Ke^{bt}\) (not a root)\(Ae^{bt}\); if \(b\) is a root, \(Ate^{bt}\)
\(K\cos\omega t\) or \(K\sin\omega t\)\(A\cos\omega t+B\sin\omega t\) (phasor method is faster)

Steps: (1) homogeneous solution; (2) particular solution; (3) add; (4) apply initial conditions to the complete solution (for second order use \(x(0^+)\) and \(\dot x(0^+)\)).
