Fourier-Transform Pairs

1.5

\(x(t)\)\(X(j\omega)\) (\(\omega\) in rad/s)\(X(f)\)
\(\delta(t)\)\(1\)\(1\)
\(1\)\(2\pi\delta(\omega)\)\(\delta(f)\)
\(e^{-at}u(t)\)\(\frac1{a+j\omega}\)\(\frac1{a+j2\pi f}\)
\(e^{-a|t|}\)\(\frac{2a}{a^2+\omega^2}\)\(\frac{2a}{a^2+4\pi^2f^2}\)
\(\rect(t/\tau)\)\(\tau\sinc\frac{\omega\tau}{2\pi}\)\(\tau\sinc(f\tau)\)
\(\tri(t/\tau)\)\(\tau\sinc^2\frac{\omega\tau}{2\pi}\)\(\tau\sinc^2(f\tau)\)
\(\sinc(Bt)\)\(\frac{2\pi}B\rect\frac{\omega}{2\pi B}\)\(\frac1B\rect\frac fB\)
\(\cos2\pi f_0t\)\(\pi[\delta(\omega-\omega_0)+\delta(\omega+\omega_0)]\)\(\frac12[\delta(f-f_0)+\delta(f+f_0)]\)
\(e^{-\pi t^2}\)\(e^{-\omega^2/4\pi}\)\(e^{-\pi f^2}\)
\(\displaystyle\sum_n\delta(t-nT)\)\(\frac{2\pi}T\sum\delta(\omega-\frac{2\pi k}T)\)\(\frac1T\sum\delta(f-\frac kT)\)

Duality: if \(x(t)\leftrightarrow X(j\omega)\) then \(X(jt)\leftrightarrow2\pi x(-\omega)\). Parseval: \(\int|x|^2dt=\frac1{2\pi}\int|X|^2d\omega=\int|X(f)|^2df\).
