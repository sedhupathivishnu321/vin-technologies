Appendix F

\(z\)-Transform Pairs and Properties

1.5

\(x[n]\)\(X(z)\)ROC
\(\delta[n]\)\(1\)all \(z\)
\(u[n]\)\(\frac1{1-z^{-1}}\)\(|z|>1\)
\(n\,u[n]\)\(\frac{z^{-1}}{(1-z^{-1})^2}\)\(|z|>1\)
\(a^nu[n]\)\(\frac1{1-az^{-1}}\)\(|z|>|a|\)
\((n+1)a^nu[n]\)\(\frac1{(1-az^{-1})^2}\)\(|z|>|a|\)
\(-a^nu[-n-1]\)\(\frac1{1-az^{-1}}\)\(|z|<|a|\)
\(\cos\Omega_0n\,u[n]\)\(\frac{1-z^{-1}\cos\Omega_0}{1-2z^{-1}\cos\Omega_0+z^{-2}}\)\(|z|>1\)
\(r^n\sin\Omega_0n\,u[n]\)\(\frac{rz^{-1}\sin\Omega_0}{1-2rz^{-1}\cos\Omega_0+r^2z^{-2}}\)\(|z|>r\)
\(a^nu[n]-a^nu[n-N]\)\(\frac{1-a^Nz^{-N}}{1-az^{-1}}\)\(|z|>0\)

Unilateral: \(x[n-1]\to z^{-1}X+x[-1]\); \(x[n-2]\to z^{-2}X+z^{-1}x[-1]+x[-2]\). Relations: \(H(e^{j\Omega})=H(z)|_{z=e^{j\Omega}}\); \(z=e^{sT}\) (sampling); bilinear \(z=\frac{1+sT/2}{1-sT/2}\).
