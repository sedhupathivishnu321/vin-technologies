Appendix L

Last-Day Formula Sheet

Circuits. \(v=iR\); \(R_{Th}=V_{oc}/I_{sc}\); \(P_{max}=V_{Th}^2/4R_{Th}\); \(Y\)–\(\Delta\): \(R_\Delta=3R_Y\) (balanced); \(x=x_\infty+(x_0-x_\infty)e^{-t/\tau}\); \(\tau=R_{Th}C=L/R_{Th}\).
AC. \(Z_L=j\omega L\), \(Z_C=\frac1{j\omega C}\); \(S=VI^*\); \(Q_C=P(\tan\phi_1-\tan\phi_2)\); \(\omega_0=\frac1{\sqrt{LC}}\); \(Q_s=\frac{\omega_0L}R\); \(BW=\frac{\omega_0}Q\); RMS: sine \(\frac{V_m}{\sqrt2}\), half-wave \(\frac{V_m}2\).
2nd order. \(\zeta=\alpha/\omega_0\); \(\%OS=100e^{-\pi\zeta/\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}}\); \(t_p=\pi/\omega_d\).
Two-port. \(A=\frac{z_{11}}{z_{21}}\), \(B=\frac{\Delta z}{z_{21}}\), \(C=\frac1{z_{21}}\), \(D=\frac{z_{22}}{z_{21}}\); \(Z_{in}=\frac{AZ_L+B}{CZ_L+D}\); reciprocal: \(AD-BC=1\).
Laplace. \(\frac1{s+a}\leftrightarrow e^{-at}\); \(f(0^+)=\lim sF\); \(f(\infty)=\lim_{s\to0}sF\).
Signals. \(E=\int|x|^2\); \(\delta(at)=\frac{\delta(t)}{|a|}\); LTI: causal \(h=0\) (\(t<0\)), stable \(\int|h|<\infty\); \(y=x*h\); \(\int y=\int x\int h\).
Fourier. \(c_k=\frac1T\int xe^{-jk\omega_0t}\); \(P=\sum|c_k|^2\); square wave \(b_n=\frac{4A}{n\pi}\); \(\rect\leftrightarrow\tau\sinc\); shift \(\to e^{-j\omega t_0}\); modulation \(\to\frac12[X(\omega\mp\omega_0)]\); Parseval \(\frac1{2\pi}\int|X|^2\).
Sampling. Nyquist rate \(2f_m\); alias \(|f-kf_s|\); product BW adds; convolution min; bandpass \(\frac{2f_H}k\le f_s\le\frac{2f_L}{k-1}\).
DT. \(a^nu\leftrightarrow\frac1{1-az^{-1}}\); causal stable: poles inside; ROC no poles; \(H(e^{j\Omega})=H(z)|_{e^{j\Omega}}\); period \(2\pi\); DFT \(X[k]\): \(N\) samples, circular convolution.
Filters. Linear phase: symmetric \(h\), \(\tau_g=\frac{N-1}2\); windows Rect/Hann/Hamming/Blackman: \(c=1.8/6.2/6.6/11\); bilinear \(s=\frac2T\frac{1-z^{-1}}{1+z^{-1}}\); \(N_{But}=\frac{\log[\ldots]}{2\log(\omega_s/\omega_p)}\).
