A. Direct conceptual questions

Set A

A1. Which of the following is a passive element? (A) VCVS (B) inductor (C) op-amp (D) independent current source.

A2. Kirchhoff’s voltage law is a statement of conservation of (A) charge (B) energy (C) momentum (D) power.

A3. Superposition can be used to find (A) voltage only (B) current only (C) voltage and current but not power (D) power.

A4. A network has \(n\) nodes. The number of independent KCL equations is (A) \(n\) (B) \(n-1\) (C) \(n+1\) (D) \(b-n+1\).

A5. In a purely capacitive AC circuit the current (A) leads the voltage by \(90^\circ\) (B) lags by \(90^\circ\) (C) is in phase (D) lags by \(180^\circ\).

A6. A capacitor voltage \(v_C\) and inductor current \(i_L\) (A) can both jump (B) cannot jump (C) \(v_C\) jumps, \(i_L\) does not (D) \(i_L\) jumps, \(v_C\) does not.

A7. Which system is time-invariant? (A) \(y=t\,x(t)\) (B) \(y=x(2t)\) (C) \(y=x^2(t)\) (D) \(y=x(-t)\).

A8. An energy signal has (A) finite energy and zero average power (B) infinite energy and finite power (C) both infinite (D) both finite and non-zero.

A9. The Fourier transform of a real and even signal is (A) real and even (B) imaginary and odd (C) real and odd (D) complex.

A10. The poles of a causal, BIBO-stable continuous-time system lie in (A) the open left half plane (B) the right half plane (C) on the \(j\omega\) axis (D) anywhere.

B. Formula-based questions

Set B

B1. \(6\ \Omega\) and \(3\ \Omega\) in parallel: (A) \(9\ \Omega\) (B) \(2\ \Omega\) (C) \(4.5\ \Omega\) (D) \(18\ \Omega\).

B2. The RMS value of a full-wave rectified sine of peak \(10\) V is (A) \(6.37\) V (B) \(7.07\) V (C) \(5\) V (D) \(10\) V.

B3. The resonant frequency of \(L=1\) mH and \(C=1\ \mu\)F is closest to (A) \(1.59\) kHz (B) \(5.03\) kHz (C) \(31.6\) kHz (D) \(159\) kHz.

B4. A series RLC has \(R=10\ \Omega\), \(L=10\) mH, \(C=10\ \mu\)F. \(Q\approx\) (A) \(0.316\) (B) \(3.16\) (C) \(31.6\) (D) \(316\).

B5. Energy stored in a \(2\) H inductor carrying \(3\) A: (A) \(3\) J (B) \(6\) J (C) \(9\) J (D) \(18\) J.

B6. Nyquist rate of a signal band-limited to \(5\) kHz: (A) \(2.5\) kHz (B) \(5\) kHz (C) \(10\) kHz (D) \(20\) kHz.

B7. The \(z\)-transform of \((0.5)^nu[n]\) is (A) \(\frac1{1-0.5z^{-1}}\) (B) \(\frac1{1-2z^{-1}}\) (C) \(\frac{z}{z+0.5}\) (D) \(\frac1{1+0.5z^{-1}}\).

B8. \(\mathcal L\{te^{-2t}u(t)\}=\) (A) \(\frac1{s+2}\) (B) \(\frac1{(s+2)^2}\) (C) \(\frac{2}{s^2}\) (D) \(\frac{s}{(s+2)^2}\).

C. Circuit reduction

Set C

C1. A balanced star of \(12\ \Omega\) resistors is converted to delta. Each delta resistor is (A) \(4\ \Omega\) (B) \(12\ \Omega\) (C) \(36\ \Omega\) (D) \(6\ \Omega\).

C2. \(3\ \Omega\) in series with (\(6\ \Omega\parallel3\ \Omega\)) equals (A) \(5\ \Omega\) (B) \(4\ \Omega\) (C) \(12\ \Omega\) (D) \(2\ \Omega\).

C3. In a balanced bridge with all arms \(10\ \Omega\) and a bridge resistor \(R_5\), \(R_{AB}=\) (A) \(5\ \Omega\) (B) \(10\ \Omega\) (C) \(20\ \Omega\) (D) depends on \(R_5\).

C4. The input resistance of an infinite ladder with series \(2\ \Omega\) and shunt \(2\ \Omega\) is (A) \(2\ \Omega\) (B) \(3.236\ \Omega\) (C) \(4\ \Omega\) (D) \(\infty\).

C5. A \(10\) V source with \(5\ \Omega\) series resistance is transformed to a current source of (A) \(2\) A in parallel with \(5\ \Omega\) (B) \(0.5\) A in parallel with \(5\ \Omega\) (C) \(2\) A in series with \(5\ \Omega\) (D) \(50\) A in parallel with \(5\ \Omega\).

C6. Three \(6\ \mu\)F capacitors in series equal (A) \(18\ \mu\)F (B) \(6\ \mu\)F (C) \(2\ \mu\)F (D) \(1/2\ \mu\)F.

D. Signal transformations

Set D

D1. \(x(2t-3)\) is obtained from \(x(t)\) by (A) compress by \(2\), delay by \(1.5\) (B) compress by \(2\), delay by \(3\) (C) expand by \(2\), delay by \(3\) (D) expand by \(2\), advance by \(1.5\).

D2. The even part of \(x(t)=e^{-t}u(t)\) at \(t=-1\) is (A) \(0\) (B) \(0.184\) (C) \(0.368\) (D) \(-0.184\).

D3. \(\delta(2t-4)=\) (A) \(2\delta(t-2)\) (B) \(\frac12\delta(t-2)\) (C) \(\delta(t-4)\) (D) \(\frac12\delta(t-4)\).

D4. \(x[n]=\{1,2,3\}\) for \(n=0,1,2\). Then \(x[1-n]\) is (A) \(\{3,2,1\}\) starting at \(n=-1\) (B) \(\{1,2,3\}\) starting at \(n=1\) (C) \(\{3,2,1\}\) starting at \(n=0\) (D) \(\{1,2,3\}\) starting at \(n=-1\).

D5. \(\cos(\pi t/3)+\sin(\pi t/2)\) has period (A) \(6\) (B) \(4\) (C) \(12\) (D) \(2\).

D6. Energy of a rectangular pulse of amplitude \(3\) and width \(2\): (A) \(6\) (B) \(9\) (C) \(18\) (D) \(12\).

E. Pole–zero questions

Set E

E1. \(H(s)=\dfrac{s+1}{(s+2)(s+3)}\) is (A) stable (B) unstable (C) marginally stable (D) non-causal.

E2. \(H(z)=\dfrac1{1-0.5z^{-1}}\) has a pole at (A) \(z=0.5\) (B) \(z=2\) (C) \(z=-0.5\) (D) \(z=0\).

E3. A causal system with poles at \(s=\pm j2\) is (A) stable (B) marginally stable (C) unstable (D) critically damped.

E4. A causal DT system with a pole at \(z=-1.2\) is (A) stable (B) marginal (C) unstable (D) FIR.

E5. An all-pass DT filter with a pole at \(a\) has a zero at (A) \(a\) (B) \(-a\) (C) \(1/a\) (D) \(0\).

E6. \(H(s)=\dfrac{s(s+1)}{s^2+4s+5}\) has zeros at (A) \(0,-1\) (B) \(0,1\) (C) \(-2\pm j\) (D) none.

F. Sampling calculations

Set F

F1. A signal with maximum frequency \(3\) kHz needs at least (A) \(3\) (B) \(6\) (C) \(1.5\) (D) \(12\) kHz.

F2. A \(1300\) Hz tone sampled at \(1\) kHz appears at (A) \(1300\) (B) \(700\) (C) \(300\) (D) \(100\) Hz.

F3. Minimum bandpass sampling rate for a \(70\)–\(90\) MHz signal: (A) \(180\) (B) \(90\) (C) \(45\) (D) \(40\) MHz.

F4. \(x(t)\) (\(2\) kHz) times \(y(t)\) (\(3\) kHz): Nyquist rate (A) \(6\) (B) \(10\) (C) \(5\) (D) \(12\) kHz.

F5. A zero-order hold introduces a delay of (A) \(T_s\) (B) \(T_s/2\) (C) \(2T_s\) (D) \(0\).

G. Transform problems

Set G

G1. The Fourier transform of \(\delta(t-1)\) is (A) \(e^{-j\omega}\) (B) \(e^{j\omega}\) (C) \(1\) (D) \(2\pi\delta(\omega-1)\).

G2. If \(F(s)=\frac1{s+1}\) then \(f(0^+)=\) (A) \(0\) (B) \(1\) (C) \(\infty\) (D) \(-1\).

G3. DTFT magnitude of \((0.5)^nu[n]\) at \(\Omega=0\) is (A) \(0.5\) (B) \(1\) (C) \(2\) (D) \(\infty\).

G4. \(\mathcal Z\{u[n-1]\}=\) (A) \(\frac{1}{1-z^{-1}}\) (B) \(\frac{z^{-1}}{1-z^{-1}}\) (C) \(\frac{z}{z-1}\) (D) \(z^{-1}\).

G5. Fourier-series coefficient \(c_1\) of \(\cos\omega_0t\) is (A) \(1\) (B) \(\frac12\) (C) \(2\) (D) \(0\).

G6. \(\mathcal L^{-1}\left\{\frac1{s(s+1)}\right\}=\) (A) \(e^{-t}\) (B) \(1-e^{-t}\) (C) \(1+e^{-t}\) (D) \(te^{-t}\).
