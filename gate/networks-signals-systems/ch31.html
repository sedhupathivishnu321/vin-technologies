A. Multi-step circuit analysis

Set A

A1. A \(12\) V source drives \(4\ \Omega\) in series with (\(6\ \Omega\parallel12\ \Omega\)). The power dissipated in the \(12\ \Omega\) resistor is (A) \(1.5\) W (B) \(3\) W (C) \(6\) W (D) \(12\) W.

A2. A \(10\) A source injects current into node \(A\). \(A\) connects to ground through \(5\ \Omega\) and to node \(B\) through \(5\ \Omega\); \(B\) connects to ground through \(10\ \Omega\). \(V_B=\) (A) \(25\) V (B) \(37.5\) V (C) \(50\) V (D) \(12.5\) V.

A3. Mesh 1 has a \(10\) V source, \(3\ \Omega\) own resistance and shares \(2\ \Omega\) with mesh 2, which has \(2\ \Omega\) own. Current in the shared resistor: (A) \(1.25\) A (B) \(2.5\) A (C) \(0.625\) A (D) \(3.75\) A.

A4. A source has \(V_{Th}=12\) V, \(R_{Th}=3\ \Omega\). Power in a \(6\ \Omega\) load: (A) \(8\) W (B) \(10.67\) W (C) \(12\) W (D) \(24\) W.

A5. Bridge: \(R_1=3\), \(R_2=6\), \(R_3=6\), \(R_4=3\), \(R_5=3\ \Omega\) (arms A–C, C–B, A–D, D–B; \(R_5\) C–D). \(R_{AB}=\) (A) \(3.6\) (B) \(4.2\) (C) \(4.5\) (D) \(6\ \Omega\).

Solutions. A1: \(I=12/(4+4)=1.5\) A; \(V_{12}=6\) V; \(P=36/12=3\) W (B). A2: \(V_A=1.5V_B\); \(10=V_A/5+V_B/10=0.4V_B\Rightarrow V_B=25\) V (A). A3: \([5,-2;-2,4]\mathbf I=[10,0]\): \(I_2=I_1/2\), \(4I_1=10\), \(I_1=2.5\), \(I_2=1.25\); shared current \(=I_1-I_2=1.25\) A (A). A4: \(I=12/9\), \(P=(1.333)^2\cdot6=10.67\) W (B). A5: \(\Delta\)–Y conversion gives \(4.2\ \Omega\) (B).

B. Theorem-based problems

Set B

B1. A network has open-circuit voltage \(20\) V and short-circuit current \(4\) A. The maximum power it can deliver to a resistive load is (A) \(20\) W (B) \(80\) W (C) \(10\) W (D) \(16\) W.

B2. \(10\) V in series with \(5\ \Omega\) feeds a node; the node connects to ground through \(5\ \Omega\) and also receives a \(2\) A source. The node voltage is (A) \(5\) V (B) \(7.5\) V (C) \(10\) V (D) \(15\) V.

B3. \(\tilde V_{Th}=12\angle0^\circ\) V (rms), \(Z_{Th}=3+j4\ \Omega\). Maximum load power is (A) \(6\) W (B) \(12\) W (C) \(24\) W (D) \(48\) W.

B4. \(V_{oc}=4\) V, \(I_{sc}=5\) A for a network with a dependent source. Its Thevenin resistance is (A) \(0.8\ \Omega\) (B) \(1.25\ \Omega\) (C) \(1.33\ \Omega\) (D) \(4\ \Omega\).

Solutions. B1: \(R_{Th}=5\ \Omega\), \(P_{max}=20^2/20=20\) W (A). B2: \(10\) V alone: \(5\) V; \(2\) A alone: \(2\times2.5=5\) V; total \(10\) V (C). B3: \(144/(4\cdot3)=12\) W (B). B4: \(R_{Th}=4/5=0.8\) (A).

C. RLC transient analysis

Set C

C1. \(H(s)=25/(s^2+6s+25)\). Percentage overshoot of the step response: (A) \(4.3\%\) (B) \(9.5\%\) (C) \(16.3\%\) (D) \(25\%\).

C2. A series RLC with \(L=4\) mH, \(C=1\ \mu\)F is critically damped for \(R=\) (A) \(63.2\ \Omega\) (B) \(126.5\ \Omega\) (C) \(200\ \Omega\) (D) \(400\ \Omega\).

C3. \(24\) V, \(R_1=4\ \Omega\), \(L=2\) H, and \(R_2=8\ \Omega\) (initially shorted) is inserted at \(t=0\). The current at \(t=0.1\) s is (A) \(2\) A (B) \(4.2\) A (C) \(6\) A (D) \(3.1\) A.

Solutions. C1: \(\omega_n=5\), \(\zeta=0.6\), \(\%OS=9.5\%\) (B). C2: \(2\sqrt{L/C}=2\sqrt{4000}=126.5\) (B). C3: \(i=2+4e^{-6t}\), \(i(0.1)=4.2\) A (B).

D. Laplace-domain circuits

Set D

D1. \(\mathcal L^{-1}\left\{\frac{2s+3}{(s+1)(s+2)}\right\}\) at \(t=1\) is (A) \(0.50\) (B) \(0.37\) (C) \(1.0\) (D) \(0.14\).

D2. \(H(s)=\dfrac{100}{s^2+10s+100}\). Its step-response overshoot is (A) \(4.3\%\) (B) \(9.5\%\) (C) \(16.3\%\) (D) \(25\%\).

D3. \(H(s)=\dfrac{s+4}{s^2+5s+6}\). The final value of the step response is (A) \(0.667\) (B) \(1\) (C) \(4\) (D) \(0\).

Solutions. D1: \(e^{-t}+e^{-2t}\); \(0.368+0.135=0.503\) (A). D2: \(\omega_n=10\), \(2\zeta\omega_n=10\), \(\zeta=0.5\), \(16.3\%\) (C). D3: \(H(0)=4/6=0.667\) (A).

E. Convolution

Set E

E1. \(\{1,2,1\}*\{1,1,1\}\) at \(n=2\): (A) \(1\) (B) \(3\) (C) \(4\) (D) \(2\).

E2. \(x(t)=u(t)-u(t-2)\): \((x*x)(2)=\) (A) \(1\) (B) \(2\) (C) \(4\) (D) \(0\).

E3. \(e^{-t}u(t)*e^{-2t}u(t)\) at \(t=\ln2\): (A) \(0.25\) (B) \(0.5\) (C) \(0.75\) (D) \(0.125\).

Solutions. E1: \(1+2+1=4\) (C). E2: triangle of base \(4\), peak \(2\) (B). E3: \(e^{-\ln2}-e^{-2\ln2}=0.5-0.25=0.25\) (A).

F. Fourier analysis

Set F

F1. A square wave of amplitude \(\pm5\) has fundamental amplitude (A) \(3.18\) (B) \(5\) (C) \(6.37\) (D) \(7.07\).

F2. \(x(t)=2+3\cos t+4\sin2t\) has average power (A) \(9\) (B) \(16.5\) (C) \(25\) (D) \(29\).

F3. The energy of \(e^{-|t|}\) is (A) \(1\) (B) \(2\) (C) \(0.5\) (D) \(\pi\).

F4. The FT of \(\rect(t/2)\cos10t\) (width-\(2\) pulse) at \(\omega=10\) is (A) \(2\) (B) \(1\) (C) \(0.5\) (D) \(0\).

Solutions. F1: \(4A/\pi=6.37\) (C). F2: \(4+\tfrac92+\tfrac{16}2=16.5\) (B). F3: \(2\int_0^\infty e^{-2t}dt=1\) (A). F4: \(\tfrac12X_r(0)=\tfrac12\cdot2=1\) (B).

G. Sampling and aliasing

Set G

G1. A \(1200\) Hz cosine is sampled at \(1\) kHz and ideally reconstructed. The output frequency is (A) \(1200\) Hz (B) \(200\) Hz (C) \(800\) Hz (D) \(0\).

G2. Nyquist rate of \(\sinc^2(1000t)\) is (A) \(1\) kHz (B) \(2\) kHz (C) \(4\) kHz (D) \(500\) Hz.

G3. Minimum sampling rate for a band-pass signal in \(5\)–\(6\) kHz: (A) \(12\) kHz (B) \(6\) kHz (C) \(2\) kHz (D) \(1\) kHz.

Solutions. G1: \(|1200-1000|=200\) (B). G2: \(\sinc(1000t)\) spectrum extends to \(500\) Hz, \(\sinc^2\) to \(1\) kHz; rate \(2\) kHz (B). G3: \(B=1\), \(\lfloor6\rfloor=6\), \(f_s=2\cdot6/6=2\) kHz (C).

H. \(z\)-transform

Set H

H1. \(X(z)=\dfrac1{(1-0.5z^{-1})^2}\) (causal). \(x[3]=\) (A) \(0.125\) (B) \(0.5\) (C) \(0.25\) (D) \(1\).

H2. \(y[n]=0.5y[n-1]+x[n]\), \(x[n]=(0.5)^nu[n]\). \(y[2]=\) (A) \(0.25\) (B) \(0.5\) (C) \(0.75\) (D) \(1\).

H3. \(H(z)=\dfrac1{(1-0.5z^{-1})(1-2z^{-1})}\) is BIBO stable for the ROC (A) \(|z|>2\) (B) \(0.5<|z|<2\) (C) \(|z|<0.5\) (D) none.

Solutions. H1: \(x[n]=(n+1)0.5^n\), \(x[3]=4\cdot0.125=0.5\) (B). H2: \(y=(n+1)0.5^n\); \(y[2]=0.75\) (C). H3: unit circle in the ROC: (B).

I. Filter design

Set I

I1. A Hamming-window FIR low-pass with transition width \(0.05\pi\) needs about (A) \(33\) (B) \(66\) (C) \(133\) (D) \(265\) taps.

I2. \(H_a(s)=\dfrac1{s+3}\) mapped by the bilinear transform with \(T=1\) has its pole at (A) \(0.2\) (B) \(-0.2\) (C) \(0.05\) (D) \(-5\).

I3. Butterworth low-pass: \(\alpha_p=3\) dB at \(\omega_p=1\), \(\alpha_s=20\) dB at \(\omega_s=2\). Minimum order: (A) \(3\) (B) \(4\) (C) \(5\) (D) \(6\).

Solutions. I1: \(6.6\pi/0.05\pi=132\to133\) (C). I2: \(z=\frac{1+sT/2}{1-sT/2}=\frac{1-1.5}{1+1.5}=-0.2\) (B). I3: \(N\ge\frac{\log(99/0.995)}{2\log2}=3.32\to4\) (B).
