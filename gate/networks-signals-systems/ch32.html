32.1 Circuit NAT problems

Set 32.1

N1. A cube is made of twelve \(6\ \Omega\) resistors. The resistance between opposite (body-diagonal) corners is  \(\Omega\).

N2. A \(12\) V source with \(4\ \Omega\) internal resistance drives a \(4\ \Omega\) load. Load power =  W.

N3. An \(8\) A source feeds a node having \(4\ \Omega\) and \(12\ \Omega\) to ground, and also a \(6\ \Omega\) resistor in series with \(3\ \Omega\) to ground. The node voltage is  V.

N4. \(R_{Th}\) of a network consisting of \(6\ \Omega\) in parallel with (\(2\ \Omega\) in series with \(4\ \Omega\)), seen at its terminals:  \(\Omega\).

N5. \(24\) V is applied to \(6\ \Omega\) in series with (\(3\ \Omega\parallel6\ \Omega\)). The power in the \(3\ \Omega\) resistor is  W.

Answers: N1: \(5R/6=5\). N2: \(I=1.5\) A, \(P=9\) W. N3: \(4\parallel12=3\); \(6+3=9\); \(3\parallel9=2.25\); \(V=8\times2.25=18\). N4: \(6\parallel6=3\). N5: \(3\parallel6=2\), \(I=24/8=3\) A, \(V=6\) V, \(P=36/3=12\) W.

32.2 AC power NAT

Set 32.2

N6. \(2\) kW load at \(0.8\) pf lagging on \(230\) V, \(50\) Hz is corrected to \(0.95\) lagging. Capacitor:  \(\mu\)F (nearest integer).

N7. \(Z=3+j4\ \Omega\) carries \(5\) A rms. Real power:  W.

N8. \(\tilde V_{Th}=10\angle0^\circ\) V (peak), \(Z_{Th}=2+j2\ \Omega\). Maximum load power:  W.

N9. A series RLC (\(R=5\ \Omega\)) at resonance is driven by \(10\) V rms. Power:  W.

Answers: N6: \(Q_1=2(0.75)=1.5\) kvar; \(\tan\phi_2=0.3287\); \(Q_C=0.8426\) kvar; \(C=842.6/(2\pi\cdot50\cdot230^2)=50.7\to51\ \mu\)F. N7: \(25\times3=75\). N8: \(100/(8\cdot2)=6.25\). N9: \(100/5=20\).

32.3 Transient NAT

Set 32.3

N10. An RC circuit (\(R=10\) k\(\Omega\), \(C=1\ \mu\)F) charges toward \(5\) V from \(0\). The time to reach \(4\) V is  ms.

N11. \(20\) V is applied at \(t=0\) to \(R=10\ \Omega\), \(L=1\) H in series (\(i(0)=0\)). \(i(0.1\text{ s})=\)  A.

N12. A series RLC has \(R=1\ \Omega\), \(L=1\) H, \(C=1\) F. Step-response percentage overshoot: %.

N13. Second-order system with \(\omega_n=10\) rad/s, \(\zeta=0.6\): peak time \(=\)  s.

Answers: N10: \(\tau=10\) ms; \(4=5(1-e^{-t/\tau})\Rightarrow t=\tau\ln5=16.1\) ms. N11: \(2(1-e^{-1})=1.264\). N12: \(\zeta=0.5\), \(16.3\%\). N13: \(\omega_d=8\), \(t_p=\pi/8=0.393\).

32.5 Sampling NAT

Set 32.5

N18. A \(2.7\) kHz tone is sampled at \(2\) kHz. Apparent frequency:  kHz.

N19. Nyquist rate of \(\cos200\pi t+\cos600\pi t\):  Hz.

N20. Minimum bandpass sampling rate for \(40\)–\(46\) kHz:  kHz (two decimals).

N21. Zero-order-hold gain at \(f_s/2\) relative to DC:  dB (two decimals).

Answers: N18: \(|2.7-2|=0.7\). N19: \(f_m=300\) Hz, rate \(600\). N20: \(B=6\), \(\lfloor46/6\rfloor=7\); \(f_s=2\cdot46/7=13.14\) (range \(13.14\ldots13.33\)). N21: \(20\log_{10}\sinc(0.5)=20\log_{10}0.6366=-3.92\).
