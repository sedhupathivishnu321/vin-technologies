How to use

MSQ: one or more options correct; no partial credit for a wrong option, and marks are awarded only when the exact correct set is chosen. Judge each option separately as True/False. \(24\) original problems with reasons.

33.1 Conceptual MSQs

Set 33.1

Q1. For a linear circuit: (A) superposition holds for voltages and currents (B) superposition holds for power (C) a Thevenin equivalent exists at any two terminals (D) reciprocity always holds.

Q2. Energy signals: (A) \(e^{-2t}u(t)\) (B) \(\rect(t)\) (C) \(\sin t\) (D) \(u(t)\).

Q3. Even functions: (A) \(\cos3t\) (B) \(t\sin t\) (C) \(t^3\) (D) \(|t|\).

Q4. Quantities that cannot change instantaneously in a circuit with finite sources: (A) \(v_C\) (B) \(i_L\) (C) \(i_C\) (D) \(v_R\).

Answers: Q1: (A), (C). (B) power is quadratic; (D) fails with dependent sources. Q2: (A), (B). Q3: (A), (B), (D). Q4: (A), (B).

33.2 Circuit-property MSQs

Set 33.2

Q5. Reciprocity holds for (A) linear bilateral passive networks with one source (B) networks with dependent sources (C) networks with diodes (D) a resistive T-network.

Q6. A two-port is symmetric and reciprocal. Then (A) \(z_{11}=z_{22}\) (B) \(z_{12}=z_{21}\) (C) \(A=D\) (D) \(AD-BC=1\).

Q7. At series RLC resonance: (A) \(|V_L|=|V_C|\) (B) power factor is unity (C) \(|Z|\) is minimum (D) current is minimum.

Q8. Maximum power transfer from a DC Thevenin source: (A) \(R_L=R_{Th}\) (B) efficiency \(50\%\) (C) \(P=V_{Th}^2/4R_{Th}\) (D) efficiency is maximum.

Answers: Q5: (A), (D). Q6: all four. Q7: (A), (B), (C). Q8: (A), (B), (C).

33.3 LTI-system MSQs

Set 33.3

Q9. An LTI system is causal if (A) \(h(t)=0\) for \(t<0\) (B) the ROC of \(H(s)\) is to the right of its rightmost pole (C) the output depends only on present and past inputs (D) all poles are in the left half-plane.

Q10. Time-invariant systems: (A) \(y=x(t-2)\) (B) \(y=x(2t)\) (C) \(y=x^2(t)\) (D) \(y=t\,x(t)\).

Q11. Linear systems: (A) \(y=x+2\) (B) \(y=dx/dt\) (C) \(y=\int_{-\infty}^tx\,d\tau\) (D) \(y=x^2\).

Q12. Convolution: (A) \(x*h=h*x\) (B) \(\int(x*h)=\int x\int h\) (C) \(x*(h_1+h_2)=x*h_1+x*h_2\) (D) duration of \(x*h\) is the larger of the two durations.

Answers: Q9: (A), (B), (C); (D) is a stability condition. Q10: (A), (C). Q11: (B), (C). Q12: (A), (B), (C); durations add.

33.4 Stability and causality MSQs

Set 33.4

Q13. Causal DT systems that are BIBO stable: (A) \(\frac1{1-0.5z^{-1}}\) (B) \(\frac1{1+0.9z^{-1}}\) (C) \(\frac1{1-1.1z^{-1}}\) (D) \(\frac1{1-z^{-1}}\).

Q14. Causal CT systems that are BIBO stable: (A) \(\frac1{s+1}\) (B) \(\frac1{s-1}\) (C) \(\frac1{s^2+4}\) (D) \(\frac1{s^2+2s+5}\).

Q15. Non-causal impulse responses: (A) \(e^{-|t|}\) (B) \(e^{-t}u(t)\) (C) \(\delta(t+1)\) (D) \(u(t-1)\).

Q16. Regarding the ROC of the \(z\)-transform: (A) a causal sequence has an exterior ROC (B) stability requires the unit circle in the ROC (C) the ROC may contain poles (D) finite-duration sequences have ROC covering all \(z\) except possibly \(0\), \(\infty\).

Answers: Q13: (A), (B). Q14: (A), (D). Q15: (A), (C). Q16: (A), (B), (D).

33.5 Transform MSQs

Set 33.5

Q17. Fourier transform: (A) \(x(t-t_0)\leftrightarrow e^{-j\omega t_0}X\) (B) time shift leaves \(|X|\) unchanged (C) \(|X|\) is even for real \(x\) (D) \(X(t)\leftrightarrow2\pi x(-\omega)\).

Q18. The final-value theorem can be applied to (A) \(\frac1{s(s+1)}\) (B) \(\frac1{s^2+1}\) (C) \(\frac1{s(s-1)}\) (D) \(\frac1{s+2}\).

Q19. \(\frac1{1-az^{-1}}\) is the \(z\)-transform of (A) \(a^nu[n]\) with \(|z|>|a|\) (B) \(-a^nu[-n-1]\) with \(|z|<|a|\) (C) \(a^nu[-n]\) (D) \(a^{|n|}\).

Q20. DTFT: (A) periodic with \(2\pi\) (B) \(|X|\) even for real \(x\) (C) convolution \(\leftrightarrow\) product (D) never contains impulses.

Answers: Q17: all four. Q18: (A), (D) (poles of \(sX\) in the LHP; (B) on the axis, (C) in the RHP). Q19: (A), (B). Q20: (A), (B), (C).

33.6 Filter MSQs

Set 33.6

Q21. Linear-phase FIR: (A) symmetric \(h\) gives linear phase (B) Type III has zeros at \(z=\pm1\) (C) group delay \(=(N-1)/2\) (D) Type II can be a high-pass.

Q22. Windows: (A) Blackman has the widest main lobe of the standard windows (B) rectangular has the narrowest (C) Hamming peak sidelobe \(\approx-41\) dB (D) Hann peak sidelobe \(\approx-13\) dB.

Q23. IIR design: (A) bilinear transform avoids aliasing (B) impulse invariance maps \(\Omega=\omega T\) but aliases (C) Butterworth is equiripple (D) Chebyshev-I needs a lower order than Butterworth for the same specification.

Q24. Structures: (A) direct form II uses \(\max(M,N)\) delays (B) cascade forms are less sensitive to coefficient quantisation (C) FIR filters are unconditionally stable (D) IIR filters can have exactly linear phase (causal, real, stable).

Answers: Q21: (A), (B), (C). Q22: (A), (B), (C); Hann is \(-31\) dB. Q23: (A), (B), (D). Q24: (A), (B), (C); causal stable IIR filters cannot have exactly linear phase.
