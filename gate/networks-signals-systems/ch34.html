Circuit-analysis formulas

1.35

Series / parallel\(R_s=\sum R\); \(G_p=\sum G\); \(C_s^{-1}=\sum C^{-1}\); \(L_p^{-1}=\sum L^{-1}\)
Dividers\(v_k=v\frac{R_k}{\sum R}\); \(i_1=i\frac{R_2}{R_1+R_2}\)
KCL/KVL counts\(n-1\) KCL, \(b-n+1\) KVL; twigs \(n-1\); links \(b-n+1\)
Nodal / mesh\(\mathbf G\mathbf V=\mathbf I\); \(\mathbf R\mathbf I=\mathbf V\)
Source transformation\(V_s=I_sR_s\)
Star–delta\(R_\Delta=\frac{\sum R_YR_Y}{R_Y^{\,opp}}\); \(R_Y=\frac{R_{\Delta}R_{\Delta}}{\sum R_\Delta}\); balanced \(R_\Delta=3R_Y\)
Bridge balance\(R_1/R_3=R_2/R_4\)
Cube (\(R\) per edge)\(\frac7{12}R\) (edge), \(\frac34R\) (face diag.), \(\frac56R\) (body diag.)
Infinite ladder\(R_{in}=R_s+R_p\parallel R_{in}\); equal \(R\): \(1.618R\)

RLC formulas

1.35

Laplace transforms

1.35

\(\delta(t)\): \(1\)\(u(t)\): \(\frac1s\)\(t\): \(\frac1{s^2}\)\(e^{-at}\): \(\frac1{s+a}\)
\(te^{-at}\): \(\frac1{(s+a)^2}\)\(\sin\omega t\): \(\frac{\omega}{s^2+\omega^2}\)\(\cos\omega t\): \(\frac{s}{s^2+\omega^2}\)\(e^{-at}\sin\omega t\): \(\frac{\omega}{(s+a)^2+\omega^2}\)
\(e^{-at}\cos\omega t\): \(\frac{s+a}{(s+a)^2+\omega^2}\)\(x(t-t_0)u\): \(e^{-st_0}X\)\(x'\): \(sX-x(0^-)\)\(\int x\): \(X/s\)
IVT: \(\lim_{s\to\infty}sX\)FVT: \(\lim_{s\to0}sX\)\(tx\): \(-X'\)\(e^{-at}x\): \(X(s+a)\)

Fourier-series formulas

\(a_0=\frac1T\int x\); \(a_n=\frac2T\int x\cos n\omega_0t\); \(b_n=\frac2T\int x\sin n\omega_0t\); \(c_k=\frac1T\int xe^{-jk\omega_0t}\); \(c_k=\frac{a_k-jb_k}2\); \(P=\sum|c_k|^2\). Square wave \(\pm A\): \(b_n=\frac{4A}{n\pi}\) (odd); triangle: \(a_n=\frac{8A}{n^2\pi^2}\) (odd); pulse train: \(c_k=\frac{A\tau}{T}\sinc\frac{k\tau}{T}\); impulse train: \(c_k=\frac1T\).

Fourier-transform pairs

1.35

\(\delta(t)\): \(1\)\(1\): \(2\pi\delta(\omega)\)\(e^{-at}u\): \(\frac1{a+j\omega}\)\(e^{-a|t|}\): \(\frac{2a}{a^2+\omega^2}\)
\(\rect(t/\tau)\): \(\tau\sinc\frac{\omega\tau}{2\pi}\)\(\tri\): \(\tau\sinc^2\)\(\frac{\sin Wt}{\pi t}\): \(\rect\frac{\omega}{2W}\)\(e^{-at^2}\): \(\sqrt{\frac\pi a}e^{-\omega^2/4a}\)
\(\cos\omega_0t\): \(\pi[\delta_++\delta_-]\)\(\mathrm{sgn}\): \(\frac2{j\omega}\)\(u\): \(\pi\delta+\frac1{j\omega}\)shift: \(e^{-j\omega t_0}\)

Properties: scaling \(\frac1{|a|}X(\omega/a)\); modulation \(\frac12[X(\omega\mp\omega_0)]\); \(d/dt\to j\omega\); \(*\leftrightarrow\times\); Parseval \(E=\frac1{2\pi}\int|X|^2\).

Sampling formulas

\(X_s=\frac1{T_s}\sum X(\omega-k\omega_s)\); Nyquist rate \(2f_m\); alias \(f_a=|f-kf_s|\); product BW adds, convolution min; bandpass \(\frac{2f_H}k\le f_s\le\frac{2f_L}{k-1}\); ZOH \(T_s\sinc(fT_s)e^{-j\pi fT_s}\); reconstruction gain \(T_s\).

DTFT pairs

\(\delta[n]\): \(1\); \(a^nu[n]\): \(\frac1{1-ae^{-j\Omega}}\); \(\delta[n-n_0]\): \(e^{-j\Omega n_0}\); \(\frac{\sin Wn}{\pi n}\): LPF; \((-1)^nx[n]\): \(X(\Omega-\pi)\); Parseval \(\sum|x|^2=\frac1{2\pi}\int|X|^2\); period \(2\pi\); \(H=\frac{\sum b_me^{-j\Omega m}}{\sum a_ke^{-j\Omega k}}\).

\(z\)-transform pairs

1.35

\(\delta[n]\): \(1\)\(u[n]\): \(\frac1{1-z^{-1}}\)\(a^nu\): \(\frac1{1-az^{-1}}\)\(na^nu\): \(\frac{az^{-1}}{(1-az^{-1})^2}\)
\(-a^nu[-n-1]\): same, \(|z|<|a|\)shift: \(z^{-n_0}\)\(a^nx\): \(X(z/a)\)\(nx\): \(-zX'\)
IVT: \(x[0]=X(\infty)\)FVT: \(\lim(1-z^{-1})X\)conv: \(XY\)\(\cos\Omega_0n\): \(\frac{1-z^{-1}\cos\Omega_0}{1-2z^{-1}\cos\Omega_0+z^{-2}}\)

FIR / IIR formulas

Linear phase: \(h[n]=\pm h[N-1-n]\), \(\tau_g=\frac{N-1}2\); Types I–IV; \(h_d=\frac{\sin\Omega_c(n-M)}{\pi(n-M)}\); Hamming \(0.54-0.46\cos\frac{2\pi n}{N-1}\); \(N\approx c\pi/\Delta\Omega\) (\(c=1.8,6.2,6.6,11\)). Impulse invariance \(z=e^{pT}\); bilinear \(s=\frac2T\frac{1-z^{-1}}{1+z^{-1}}\), \(\omega=\frac2T\tan\frac\Omega2\); Butterworth \(N=\frac{\log[(10^{.1\alpha_s}-1)/(10^{.1\alpha_p}-1)]}{2\log(\omega_s/\omega_p)}\); Chebyshev \(N=\frac{\cosh^{-1}\sqrt{[\ldots]}}{\cosh^{-1}(\omega_s/\omega_p)}\).

Two-port formulas

\(\mathbf Y=\mathbf Z^{-1}\); \(A=z_{11}/z_{21}\), \(B=\Delta_z/z_{21}\), \(C=1/z_{21}\), \(D=z_{22}/z_{21}\); cascade \(\mathbf T_a\mathbf T_b\); \(Z_{in}=\frac{AZ_L+B}{CZ_L+D}\); \(\frac{V_2}{V_1}=\frac{Z_L}{AZ_L+B}\).

Signal energy/power

\(E=\int|x|^2\); \(P=\lim\frac1{2T}\int|x|^2\); \(A\cos\omega t\): \(P=A^2/2\); \(u(t)\): \(P=\frac12\); \(e^{-at}u\): \(E=\frac1{2a}\); \((0.5)^nu[n]\): \(E=\frac43\); \(\delta(at)=\frac1{|a|}\delta(t)\).
