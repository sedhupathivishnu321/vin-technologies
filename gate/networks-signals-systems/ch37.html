37.1 Test 1: Circuit analysis (15 marks, 25 minutes)

Test 1: 1-mark questions

3. Time constant of \(R=5\ \Omega\) in series with \(L=10\) mH: (A) \(0.5\) ms (B) \(2\) ms (C) \(50\) ms (D) \(5\) ms.

4. Impedance of a \(0.1\) H inductor at \(100\) rad/s: (A) \(10\) (B) \(j10\) (C) \(-j10\) (D) \(0.1\) \(\Omega\).

5. A reciprocal two-port satisfies (A) \(AD-BC=0\) (B) \(AD-BC=1\) (C) \(A=D\) (D) \(B=C\).

Test 1: 2-mark questions

6. A \(6\) A source feeds a node with \(4\ \Omega\) and \(12\ \Omega\) to ground. The node voltage: (A) \(9\) V (B) \(18\) V (C) \(24\) V (D) \(72\) V.

7. A \(24\) V source with \(6\ \Omega\) internal resistance feeds a variable load. Maximum load power: (A) \(12\) W (B) \(24\) W (C) \(48\) W (D) \(96\) W.

9. A capacitor charges as \(v=8(1-e^{-t/\tau})\) V with \(R=2\) k\(\Omega\), \(C=100\ \mu\)F. Time to reach \(4\) V: (A) \(0.1\) s (B) \(0.139\) s (C) \(0.2\) s (D) \(0.4\) s.

10. A load has \(S=8+j6\) kVA. The shunt capacitor giving unity pf supplies (A) \(2\) kvar (B) \(6\) kvar (C) \(8\) kvar (D) \(10\) kvar.

37.7 Answer key and brief solutions

Answers

Test 1: 1-C, 2-B, 3-B, 4-B, 5-B, 6-B, 7-B, 8-A, 9-B, 10-B.
Test 2: 1-B, 2-B, 3-B, 4-B, 5-B, 6-C, 7-B, 8-B, 9-B, 10-C.
Test 3: 1-A, 2-B, 3-B, 4-A, 5-B, 6-B, 7-B, 8-A, 9-B, 10-C.
Test 4: 1-B, 2-B, 3-B, 4-A, 5-A, 6-B, 7-A, 8-B, 9-B, 10-A.
Test 5: 1-\(4.85\), 2-\(4\), 3-\(2.71\), 4-\(25.4\), 5-\(6.37\), 6-\(0.333\), 7-\(20\), 8-\(4\), 9-\(5\), 10-\(110\).
Test 6-A: 1-B, 2-B, 3-B, 4-A, 5-B, 6-A, 7-A, 8-A, 9-C, 10-B, 11-\(5.20\), 12-\(10\), 13-\(8\), 14-A,C, 15-A,D.
Test 6-B: 16-B, 17-B, 18-A, 19-\(8.65\), 20-C, 21-B, 22-A, 23-\(0.849\), 24-\(25.1\), 25-B, 26-A, 27-B, 28-\(4\), 29-A,C, 30-A,C,D.

Solutions (brief)

Test 1. 1: \(6+6=12\). 2: \(10/5\). 3: \(L/R=10\text{ mH}/5=2\) ms. 4: \(j\omega L=j10\). 5: reciprocity. 6: \(4\parallel12=3\), \(6\times3=18\). 7: \(24^2/(4\cdot6)=24\). 8: \(R/L=50\). 9: \(\tau=0.2\), \(t=\tau\ln2=0.139\). 10: \(Q=6\) kvar.
Test 2. 1: periods \(0.5\), \(1/3\): \(T=1\). 2: \(\int e^{-6t}=1/6\). 3: \(2f_m=4\). 4: standard. 5: affine. 6: \(\{1,3,3,1\}\), \(y[2]=3\). 7: \(sF\to2/4\). 8: \(1+\frac12\). 9: \(1/(1+0.8)=0.556\). 10: \(x^2\) has \(6\) kHz; rate \(12\) kHz.
Test 3. 1: \(I_N=5\) A. 2: \(8-5+1\). 3: \(\frac12\cdot2\times10^{-6}\cdot10^4=10\) mJ. 4: DC short. 5: \(2\pi\delta(\omega)\). 6: \(1/\tau=500\) Hz. 7: \(z^{-2}\). 8: \(|p|<1\). 9: sum \(=4\). 10: \((N-1)/2=4\).
Test 4. 1: the current through the \(2\ \Omega\) from \(A\) to \(B\) equals \(V_B/2\) (it all flows to ground through \(B\)’s \(2\ \Omega\)), so \(V_A-V_B=2\cdot V_B/2=V_B\), i.e. \(V_A=2V_B\). KCL at \(A\): \(6=V_A/4+V_B/2=0.5V_B+0.5V_B=V_B\), so \(V_B=6\) V. 2: \(10=15\cdot5/(R+5)\Rightarrow R=2.5\). 3: \(Z=6+j8\), \(I=2\), \(P=4\cdot6=24\). 4: \(3(1-e^{-1})=1.9\). 5: \(\frac12(e^{-1}-e^{-3})=0.159\). 6: \(r(t)-r(t-1)=1.5-0.5=1\). 7: \(\frac{4}2+\frac{16}2=10\). 8: \(|450-500|=50\). 9: \(0.5^{n-1}\) at \(n=3\): \(0.25\). 10: \(z=\frac{1-0.5}{1+0.5}=\frac13\).
Test 5. 1: \(1.618\times3=4.85\). 2: \(S=5\), \(Q=4\). 3: \(20e^{-2}=2.71\). 4: \(\alpha=2\), \(\omega_0=5\), \(\zeta=0.4\): \(100e^{-\pi0.4/\sqrt{0.84}}=25.4\). 5: \(20/\pi\). 6: \(1/3\). 7: \(B=10\), \(\lfloor110/10\rfloor=11\), \(f_s=220/11=20\). 8: \(1+2+1=4\). 9: \(0.5/0.1=5\). 10: \(11\pi/0.1\pi=110\).
Test 6-A. 3: \(1/(2\pi50\cdot10^{-5})=318\). 6: odd integrand. 9: \(2f_m=20\). 11: \(9/\sqrt3=5.196\). 12: \(1+4+4+1=10\). 13: \(2\pi/0.25\pi=8\). 14: (A),(C). 15: a high-pass needs \(H(e^{j\pi})\ne0\); Types II and III have a zero at \(z=-1\), so only Types I and IV qualify: (A),(D).
Test 6-B. 16: \(12\times3/9=4\). 17: \(2\tan\phi=2(0.75)=1.5\). 18: \(1/RC=500\). 19: \(\tau=0.1\): \(10(1-e^{-2})=8.65\). 20: \(\lim sF=1\). 21: \(\begin{bmatrix}1&3\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\1/3&1\end{bmatrix}\): \(A=1+1=2\). 22: \(y=\{1,1,1,-3\}\), \(y[3]=-3\). 23: \(8/(3\pi)=0.849\). 24: \(2\pi E=2\pi\cdot4=25.1\). 25: aliases add: \(2\cos2\pi100t\). 26: \(2(1-0.5^3)=1.75\). 27: \(\cos(\pi/4)=0.707\). 28: ratio \(\dfrac{10^{3}-1}{10^{0.2}-1}=1708\), \(N=\dfrac{\log_{10}1708}{2\log_{10}3}=3.39\to4\). 29: poles \(0.9\); \(-1.1\) (unstable); \(|p|=0.5\); \(1\) (marginal): (A),(C). 30: (A),(C),(D).
