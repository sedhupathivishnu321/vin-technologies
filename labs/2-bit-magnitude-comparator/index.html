2-bit magnitude comparator

Digital Systems Lab · free online experiment, runs in your browser with no install or sign-up.

Open this experiment in the circuit simulator

Objective

Compare two 2-bit numbers with gates and read A>B, A=B and A<B.

Procedure

  1. Run through all 16 input pairs and watch the three outputs.

Theory

A=B when both bit pairs are equal (XNOR · XNOR). A>B when A1·B1' or (A1 ≡ B1)·A0·B0'. A<B is the mirror image.

Questions to answer

  1. How is it extended to 4 or 8 bits?

Components used

7 × AND, 3 × Logic probe / LED, 4 × Logic switch, 4 × NOT, 2 × OR, 2 × XNOR

About the model

Ideal wires; component models as listed in the catalogue (no parasitics, no temperature drift). Built without a breadboard so the schematic stays readable.
