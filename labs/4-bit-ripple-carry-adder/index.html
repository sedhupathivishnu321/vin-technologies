4-bit ripple-carry adder

Digital Systems Lab · free online experiment, runs in your browser with no install or sign-up.

Open this experiment in the circuit simulator

Objective

Chain four full adders and add 7 + 5, 9 + 9, 15 + 1 and 6 + 3 (one per second).

Procedure

  1. Run and read S3…S0 and C4 for each of the four additions.

Theory

The carry ripples from bit 0 to bit 3: S = A + B (5 bits including the final carry C4). The delay grows with every stage.

Questions to answer

  1. What limits the speed of a ripple-carry adder?

Components used

4 × Full adder, 5 × Logic probe / LED, 9 × Logic switch

About the model

Ideal wires; component models as listed in the catalogue (no parasitics, no temperature drift). Built without a breadboard so the schematic stays readable.
