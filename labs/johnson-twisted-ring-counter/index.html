Johnson (twisted-ring) counter

Digital Systems Lab · free online experiment, runs in your browser with no install or sign-up.

Open this experiment in the circuit simulator

Objective

Feed the inverted last stage back to the first: a 4-bit register gives an 8-state sequence with only one bit changing at a time.

Procedure

  1. Run and read Q3 Q2 Q1 Q0 after each edge.

Theory

D = NOT Q3. The register fills with 1s and then with 0s: 0000, 0001, 0011, 0111, 1111, 1110, 1100, 1000. n flip-flops give 2n states.

Questions to answer

  1. Why is a Johnson counter easy to decode?

Components used

4-bit shift register, Clock source, 4 × Logic probe / LED, NOT

About the model

Ideal wires; component models as listed in the catalogue (no parasitics, no temperature drift). Built without a breadboard so the schematic stays readable.
