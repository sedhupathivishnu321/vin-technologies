NOR as a universal gate

Digital Systems Lab · free online experiment, runs in your browser with no install or sign-up.

Open this experiment in the circuit simulator

Objective

Build NOT, OR and AND from NOR gates only and verify their truth tables.

Procedure

  1. Run and read the three probes (NOT A, A+B, A·B).

Theory

NOT A = NOR(A, A). OR = NOT(NOR(A, B)). AND = NOR(NOT A, NOT B).

Questions to answer

  1. What is the dual of NAND universality?

Components used

3 × Logic probe / LED, 2 × Logic switch, 5 × NOR

About the model

Ideal wires; component models as listed in the catalogue (no parasitics, no temperature drift). Built without a breadboard so the schematic stays readable.
