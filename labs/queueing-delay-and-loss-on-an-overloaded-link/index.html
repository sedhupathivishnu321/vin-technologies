Queueing delay and loss on an overloaded link

Computer Networks Lab · free online experiment, runs in your browser with no install or sign-up.

Open this experiment in the network simulator

Objective

Offer 12 Mbit/s of UDP to a 10 Mbit/s link with a 20-packet queue: the queue fills, delay rises and about 1/6 of the packets are dropped.

Procedure

  1. Run and read the UDP loss and delay in the Summary.
  2. Select the bottleneck link and raise the queue to 200 packets: delay grows, loss shrinks (for a short burst).
  3. Lower the offered rate to 8 Mbit/s: no loss.

Theory

When the arrival rate exceeds the service rate the queue grows until it is full; then the excess is dropped (tail drop). Loss ≈ (λ − μ)/λ = 16.7 % and the queueing delay saturates at queue length × packet time.

Network blocks used

2 × Host / PC, Router
