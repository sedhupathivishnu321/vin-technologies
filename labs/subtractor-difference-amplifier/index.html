Subtractor (difference amplifier)

Analog Circuits Lab · free online experiment, runs in your browser with no install or sign-up.

Open this experiment in the circuit simulator

Objective

Subtract two voltages: Vout = (Rf/R1)·(V2 − V1).

Procedure

  1. Run and read Vout.
  2. Add the same offset to both inputs: Vout does not change.

Theory

With matched resistors R1 = R3 = 10 kΩ and Rf = R4 = 20 kΩ the output is 2·(V2 − V1) = 2·(2 V − 1 V) = 2 V, and a common-mode signal is rejected.

Questions to answer

  1. What limits the common-mode rejection in practice?

Components used

4 × Battery / DC source, Op-Amp LM741, 4 × Resistor 220 ohm 1/4W

About the model

Ideal wires; component models as listed in the catalogue (no parasitics, no temperature drift). Built without a breadboard so the schematic stays readable.
