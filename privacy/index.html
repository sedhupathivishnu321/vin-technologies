Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026. Applies to vintechnologies.co.in, including the LEARNERS LAB circuit, network and 8085/8051 trainer simulators.

In short

You can use every simulator without an account. Your work stays in your own browser. If you choose to sign in with Google, a copy of your projects is saved in a private folder in your own Google Drive. We do not run a database of user projects and we do not receive, read or sell your files.

Data kept in your browser

The simulators store your current circuit, your trainer-kit program, your selected experiment and display preferences (such as light or dark theme) in your browser's local storage on your device. This is capped at 8 MB; when it is full the oldest saved work is removed first. You can delete it at any time with the "Clear local cache" button in the account menu or by clearing site data in your browser.

Optional Google sign-in

If you click "Sign in", Google's own sign-in window opens. LEARNERS LAB asks for:

  • your name and email address, only to show who is signed in on the page;
  • the Google Drive "application data" permission (drive.appdata), which lets LEARNERS LAB create and read one hidden file in its own app folder in your Drive. It cannot see, list or change any of your other Drive files.

Your projects are sent directly from your browser to Google Drive. They do not pass through our servers and are not stored by VIN Technologies or on GitHub. You can sign out at any time (this revokes the page's access token), and you can remove the app's access and its hidden file at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where allowed. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com and learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP address and browser type when pages are requested; see GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts, scripts or the Pyodide runtime may also be loaded from public CDNs.

Children

The simulators are educational tools for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact and changes

Questions or requests: electronlearners@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. See also the Terms of Service.
