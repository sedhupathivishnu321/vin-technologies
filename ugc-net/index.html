UGC NET Mock Test Series

Paper 1 (General) + Paper 2 Electronic Science (subject code 88) · full-length, timed, with answer keys

Each mock test is a complete UGC NET sitting: Paper 1 and Paper 2 together, answered against one timer, the way the computer-based exam is held. Before you start, you can set your own duration. If you don't, the test runs for the standard 180 minutes. When you submit, or when time runs out, you get your score for each paper, the full answer key and an explanation for every question.
