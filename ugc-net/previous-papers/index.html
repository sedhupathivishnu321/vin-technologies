Notes

  • Until 2018 the exam had three papers. From July 2018 Paper III was dropped. Since December 2018 the exam has been a computer-based test by NTA with Paper 1 (50 questions) and Paper 2 (100 questions) in one 3-hour sitting.
  • From 2009 to 2011 Paper III was descriptive (written answers). From June 2012 it was objective (75 multiple-choice questions).
  • NTA has not published the question papers from December 2018 onward on its website. Candidates could only see their own response sheets for a short period after each exam.
